General-Purpose Date and Time Classes

The CTime class provides one way to represent date and time information. The CTimeSpan class represents elapsed time, such as the difference between two CTime objects.

CTime objects can represent dates between January 1, 1970, and January 18, 2038. CTime objects have a resolution of 1 second. CTime is based on the time_t data type.
