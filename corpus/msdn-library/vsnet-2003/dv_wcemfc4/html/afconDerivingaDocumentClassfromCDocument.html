Deriving a Document Class from CDocument

Documents contain and manage the data of your application. To use the AppWizard-supplied document class, you must do the following:

  • Derive a class from CDocument for each type of document.
  • Add member variables to store the data of each document.
  • Override the Serialize member function of CDocument in your document class. Serialize writes and reads the data of the document to and from disk.

Other Document Functions Often Overridden

You may also want to override other CDocument member functions. In particular, you will often need to override OnNewDocument and OnOpenDocument to initialize the data members of the document and DeleteContents to destroy dynamically allocated data.
