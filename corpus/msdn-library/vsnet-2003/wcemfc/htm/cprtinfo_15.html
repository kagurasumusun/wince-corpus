CPrintInfo::m_rectDraw

This data member specifies the usable drawing area of the page in logical coordinates. You may want to refer to this in your override of CView::OnPrint. You can use this data member to keep track of what area remains usable after you print headers, footers, and so on. The m_rectDraw member is a public variable of type CRect.

CRect m_rectDraw;
