CCeDBEnum

This class enumerates the databases in the object store. The constructor starts the enumeration, and this process continues until all databases are enumerated or the enumerator is destroyed.

Requirements

  Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later
  Header file: Declared in Wcedb.h
  Platform: H/PC Pro, Palm-size PC, Pocket PC
