D3DGAMMARAMP

This structure contains red, green, and blue ramp data.

typedef struct _D3DGAMMARAMP {
    WORD                red  [256];
    WORD                green[256];
    WORD                blue [256];
} D3DGAMMARAMP;

Members

red, green, and blue
Array of 256 WORD elements that describe the red, green, and blue gamma ramps.
