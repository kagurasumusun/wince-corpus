Flat Shading

In the flat shading mode, the Direct3D rendering pipeline renders a polygon using the lighting intensity and the intensities of the polygon material at its first vertex as the intensities for the entire polygon. 3-D objects that are rendered with flat shading have visibly sharp edges between polygons if they are not coplanar.

The following figure shows a teapot rendered with flat shading. The outline of each polygon is clearly visible. Flat shading is computationally the least expensive form of shading.
