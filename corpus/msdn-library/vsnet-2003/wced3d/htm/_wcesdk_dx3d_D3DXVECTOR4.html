D3DXVECTOR4

This structure describes a vector in four-dimensional (4-D) space.

typedef struct D3DXVECTOR4 {
  FLOAT x, y, z, w;
} D3DXVECTOR4;

Members

x
The x component.
y
The y component.
z
The z component.
w
The w component.
