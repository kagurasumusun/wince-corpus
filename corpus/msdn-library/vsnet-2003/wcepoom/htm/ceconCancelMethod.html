Cancel Method

To send a cancellation to a meeting in the Outlook 98 object model, you must first set the MeetingStatus property to olMeetingCanceled. However, this property is read-only in POOM, so Cancel was added to send a cancellation message for a meeting. To delete the meeting from your Calendar, you must also call Delete.
