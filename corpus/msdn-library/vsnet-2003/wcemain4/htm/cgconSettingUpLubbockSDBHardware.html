Setting up the Intel DBPXA250 Development Platform Hardware

  1. Connect a keyboard and a mouse.
  2. Connect an Ethernet cable to the built-in SMC port.

    - or -

    Connect an Ethernet cable to a CF PCMCIA NE2000 network adapter. This requires NE2000 be chosen in the loader menu.

  3. Connect a serial cable to the RS232 port.

    This is the serial port closest to the front of the case labeled "FF SERIAL".

  4. Connect a power cable to the front right connector.
  5. Modify the Intel DBPXA250 development platform switches and jumpers.
