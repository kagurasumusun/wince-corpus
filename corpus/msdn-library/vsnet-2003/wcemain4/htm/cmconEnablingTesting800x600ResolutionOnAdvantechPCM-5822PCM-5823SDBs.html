Enabling and Testing the 800x600 Resolution on the Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC

In the SOURCES file, navigate to %_WINCEROOT%\Public\Common\Oak\Csp\i486\Geode\GXVideo\Base. From the CDEFINES line containing -DTV_NTSC_ENABLE, remove -DTV_NTSC_ENABLE.
