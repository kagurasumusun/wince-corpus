Waveform Audio Summary

The waveform audio API gives an application exact control over waveform audio input/output devices.

The waveform audio features also include the hardware audio mixer API. In addition to providing volume control, this API provides routing services to manage audio lines on your device for playing or recording. Routing services require specific hardware device and driver support. The mixer architecture allows several audio source lines to be mapped to a single destination audio line.
