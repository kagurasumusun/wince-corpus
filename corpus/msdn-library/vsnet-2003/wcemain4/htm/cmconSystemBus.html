System Bus

The system bus that an OEM chooses and how the platform drivers interact with this bus can have a large impact on real-time performance. The OS has no control over how drivers interact with the platform bus. A driver, through its interactions with the bus, can delay other events while an action is occurring. OEMs should examine how drivers in their platform impact real-time processing. Testing has shown that both display drivers and network drivers for the CEPC impact real-time performance because the CEPC runs on a standard PC bus compared to memory-based platform. In the CEPC, however, the shipped NIC cards are shown to have an impact on real-time threads. There are two basic types of display drivers released on the CEPC: non-hardware optimized and hardware optimized. The optimized display drivers, such as the S3 Virge and ATI, impact real-time threads because their hardware optimizations lock the bus for extended periods of time. The non-hardware optimized display drivers, such as 8PP and Flat, do not have an impact on real-time threads.
