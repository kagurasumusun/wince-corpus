I/O Device Recommendations

The following hardware-design guidelines are recommended for Windows Thin Client system I/O.

Optional Features/Peripherals to Include

  • Display hardware that is capable of 640 x 480-pixel resolution, at 8 BPP. Larger display hardware, up to 16 BPP is also acceptable. Microsoft strongly recommends graphics hardware acceleration for fast video response is.
  • A keyboard, a pointing device, and network connectivity hardware.
  • Parallel, serial, and/or USB ports.
  • Support for audio output.
  • Use of mass-storage devices such as ATA PC Cards is optional for the Windows Thin Client design. Rotating mass-storage devices, however, are not recommended due to reliability concerns.

Unsupported Features

  • Audio-input hardware.
  • IrDA and video capture.
