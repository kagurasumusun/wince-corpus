JIT Debugging

In Windows CE .NET, a just-in-time (JIT) debugger is no longer launched with a first chance exception. Instead, it is launched with a second chance exception.

The previous implementation caused a registered JIT debugger to be launched whenever there was a first chance exception. The problem with a first chance exception being caught by a debugger is that the exception may be caught by the offending application. Therefore, launching a debugger introduced unnecessary overhead to the System.edb.
