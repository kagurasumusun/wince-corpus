Downloading the OS Image to the Hitachi US7729 HARP (Keywest) SDB

  1. Turn off the Hitachi US7729 HARP (Keywest) SDB.
  2. Set DIP switch SW1 on the Hitachi US7729 HARP (Keywest) SDB to the settings shown in the table below.

    The switch is located on the backside of the Hitachi US7729 HARP (Keywest) SDB, on the left, near the power connecter.

    Switch Setting
    SW1-1 Off
    SW1-2 Off
    SW1-3 Off
    SW1-4 On
  3. Download the OS image.
