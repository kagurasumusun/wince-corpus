Modifying the NEC DDB-Vr4122 (Eagle) SDB Switches and Jumpers

  1. The following table shows the functionality of DIP switch labeled DSW1. The left side is DSW1-1, and the right side is DSW1-4.
    S1-1 S1-2 S1-3 S1-4 Function
    On Off Off Off Boot ROM monitor program.
    Off Off Off Off Jump to ROM Eboot.
    X Off Off On Jump to flash image, Eboot or Nk.
  2. Build the platform.
