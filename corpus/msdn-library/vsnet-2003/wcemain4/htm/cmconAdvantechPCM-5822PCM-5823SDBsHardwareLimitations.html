Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC Hardware Limitations

For non-PCI or builtin devices, some Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC components may need to be disabled in order to allow for IRQ support on some devices. For more information, see the Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC documentation.
