Setting Up the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit Hardware

  1. Connect a serial cable to a development workstation and to the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit serial connection labeled J16.
  2. On the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit, connect an Ethernet cable to the network connector labeled J6, and then connect the cable to a development workstation or Ethernet hub.
  3. Optionally connect an NE2000 Ethernet CF card to the top PCMCIA slot with a PCMCIA to CF adapter card.
    Note   You only need to do this when you program the media access control (MAC) address for the SMC NIC chip on the SA-1111 development module. Perform the flash memory programming and the download of the Windows CE boot loader on a separate development workstation that does not have Platform Builder installed.
  4. Connect the parallel end of the base station cable to a development workstation, and then connect the flat black connector to the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit.

    The base station cable has three plastic sheathed cables and a total of four connectors. One end has a small, flat, black plastic male connector that connects to the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit. You only need to do this when you download Eboot.nb0 to flash memory.

  5. Connect the AC/DC power converter to the board and a power supply.

    To turn off the power you need to disconnect the AC/DC power converter from the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit.

  6. Modify the Intel SA1110/SA1111 (Assabet) Development Kit switches and jumpers.
