Japanese IM Search Panels

To include a search panel IM on a Windows CE–based platform, you need to include the corresponding feature in your platform configuration. The search panels do not include or depend on the functionality of an Input Method Editor (IME). To incorporate an IME that works in conjunction with an IM in your target platform, you need to include the IME feature in addition to the IM feature in your platform configuration.
