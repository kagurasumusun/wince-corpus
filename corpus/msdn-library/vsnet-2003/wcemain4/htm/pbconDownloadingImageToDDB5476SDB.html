Downloading the OS Image to the NEC DDB-Vrc5476 (Boston) SDB

  1. Turn off the NEC DDB-Vrc5476 (Boston) SDB.
  2. Be sure DIP switch S1-1 is in the Off position.
  3. Download the OS image.
