Peripheral Access Considerations in Real-Time

As with any real-time operating system (OS), your understanding of the impact of accessing peripherals on a real-time device is important to ensure the real-time performance of that OS. When you read the complementary metal oxide semiconductor (CMOS) system clock, work with OS buses, and access different devices, the activities can have a significant impact on the responsiveness of the system.

The following topics discuss some of the issues you must consider when you design real-time OSs that access peripherals:

  • Real-Time Clock
  • System Bus
