Japanese IME98 Implementation

You can implement Japanese IME98 on your platform by simple drag-and-drop actions in Platform Builder. The following steps show how to implement IME 98 in your project.

  1. In the Catalog, navigate to Core OS\Display-based Devices\International\Locale Specific Support\Japanese\Input Method Editors and then drag and drop IME 98 to the FeatureView display of your platform.
  2. Select one of IME 98 databases.

    The Compact Database, msime98s.dic, is approximately 889 KB, and the Standard Database, msime98m.dic, is 4.7 MB.
