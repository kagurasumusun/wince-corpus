Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC

The Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 are both Geode-based single board computers manufactured by Advantech that are similar to each other. The only difference is that PCM-5822 has a single Ethernet port and PCM-5823 has dual Ethernet ports.
