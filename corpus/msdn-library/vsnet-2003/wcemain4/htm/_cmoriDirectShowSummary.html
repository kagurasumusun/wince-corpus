DirectShow Summary

The DirectShow API enables playback of multimedia streams locally, over a network, or the Internet. Many important multimedia types are supported including: MPEG audio and video standards, Audio-Video Interleaved (AVI), WAV, MIDI, and Windows Media.

DirectShow uses filters to manage and manipulate multimedia data. A DirectShow filter will perform some action, such as parsing, decoding, formatting, or rendering, on a multimedia stream. This allows the output of one filter to be directed to the input of one or more other filters in what is called a filter graph. By constructing various filter graphs using the Filter Graph Manager (FGM), you can use DirectShow as a flexible intermediary to take various kinds of input and translate them into various kinds of output depending on the needs of your application and the capabilities of your platform. You can extend DirectShow media support by writing your own filters.
