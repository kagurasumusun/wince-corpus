Enabling Product Ethernet on the Advantech PCM-5823 SDB

  1. In the integrated development environment, set DUAL_ETHER = 1 to enable, and then use the dual Ethernet ports on the Advantech PCM-5823 SDB.
  2. Rebuild the image.

    Now you can download the OS image over one Ethernet port, connect to the network, and browse the Internet over the other Ethernet port.
