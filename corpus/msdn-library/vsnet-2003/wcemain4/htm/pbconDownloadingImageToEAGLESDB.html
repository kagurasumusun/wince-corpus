Downloading the OS Image to the NEC DDB-Vr4122 (Eagle) SDB

  1. Turn off the NEC DDB-Vr4122 (Eagle) SDB.
  2. Be sure DIP switch DSW1-4 is in the On position and turn on the NEC DDB-Vr4122 (Eagle) SDB.
  3. Download the OS image.
