Replacing Ethernet KITL with Serial KITL for CEPC

For CEPC platforms that do not have an Ethernet connection, downloading and debugging capability is now available through a serial port. You can also download through a serial port, but a serial boot loader is required.

To change the default transport from Ethernet to serial

  1. Open %_WINCEROOT%\Platform\Cepc\Kernel\Hal\Halkitl.c.
  2. Find the OEMKitlInit function and replace InitEther(pKitl), which is located in the default case of the switch statement, with InitSerial(pKitl).
  3. Build the platform.
  4. Remove serial port registry settings under HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Drivers\BuiltIn\Serial from Platform.reg located in %_FLATRELEASEDIR% to disable the product serial port.
  5. From the Build menu, choose Make Image.
