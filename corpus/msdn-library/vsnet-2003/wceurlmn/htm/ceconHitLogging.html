Hit Logging

Hit logging collects information for content providers about activity on their pages by logging page hits in the browser. The logs that are created contain the URL of the resource, the browsing context, whether the information was viewed from the cache, the date viewed, the time that the viewing started, the duration it was viewed, and the custom information.
