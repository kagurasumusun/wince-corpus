RTC_AUDIO_DEVICE

This enumeration describes the possible audio devices.

typedef enum RTC_AUDIO_DEVICE{
RTCAD_SPEAKER,
RTCAD_MICROPHONE
} RTC_AUDIO_DEVICE;

Elements

RTCAD_SPEAKER
Speaker audio device.
RTCAD_MICROPHONE
Microphone audio device.
