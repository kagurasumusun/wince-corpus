ISpCFGInterpreter

ISpCFGInterpretor initializes a grammar or generates new properties and text replacements for a grammar.

Method Description
ISpCFGInterpreter::InitGrammar Initializes a grammar that is loaded from an object or DLL.
ISpCFGInterpreter::Interpret Examines a grammar and generates new properties and text replacements.
