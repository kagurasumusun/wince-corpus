CComAggObject::m_contained

A CComContainedObject object derived from your class. All IUnknown calls through m_contained are delegated to the outer unknown.

CComContainedObject< contained > m_contained;

Parameters

contained
[in] Your class, derived from CComObjectRoot or CComObjectRootEx, as well as from any other interfaces you want to support on the object.

Requirements

  Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later
  Header file: Declared in Atlcom.h
  Platform: H/PC Pro, Palm-size PC, Pocket PC
