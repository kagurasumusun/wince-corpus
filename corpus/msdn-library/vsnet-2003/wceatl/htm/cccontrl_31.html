CComControl::GetZoomInfo

This method retrieves the x and y values of the numerator and denominator of the zoom factor for a control activated for in-place editing. The zoom factor is the proportion of the natural size of the control to its current extent.

void GetZoomInfo(
ATL_DRAWINFO& di );

Parameters

di
[out] The structure that will hold the zoom factor numerator and denominator.

Requirements

  Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later
  Header file: Declared in Atlctl.h
  Platform: H/PC Pro, Palm-size PC, Pocket PC
