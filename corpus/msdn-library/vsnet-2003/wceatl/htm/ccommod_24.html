CComModule::m_hInst

This data member contains the handle to the module instance. The Init method sets m_hInst to the handle passed to DLLMain or WinMain.

HINSTANCE m_hInst;

Requirements

  Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later
  Header file: Declared in Atlbase.h
  Platform: H/PC Pro, Palm-size PC, Pocket PC
