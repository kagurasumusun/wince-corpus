CComFakeCriticalSection

This class mirrors CComCriticalSection and CComAutoCriticalSection. However, CComFakeCriticalSection does not provide a critical section; therefore, its methods do nothing.

Typically, you use CComFakeCriticalSection through a typedef name, either AutoCriticalSection or CriticalSection. When using the single threaded model, CComSingleThreadModel, or the multi-threaded model without critical sections, CComMultiThreadModelNoCS, CComCriticalSection and CComAutoCriticalSection are both defined as CComFakeCriticalSection using the typedef keyword.
