CWindow::LoadSHMenuBar

This method creates a MenuBar for the Pocket PC and loads it at the bottom of the window.

HWND LoadSHMenuBar( 
LPCTSTR lpszResourceName );

Parameters

lpszResourceName
Pointer to the name of the Menu Bar to be loaded.

Requirements

Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later
Header file: Declared in Atlwin.h
