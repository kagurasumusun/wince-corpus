CComCachedTearOffObject::m_contained

This data member is a CComContainedObject object derived from your tear-off class. The methods that m_contained inherits are used to access the tear-off interface in your tear-off class through the QueryInterface, FinalConstruct, and FinalRelease of the cached tear-off object.

CComContainedObject<contained> m_contained;

Parameters

contained
[in] Your tear-off class, derived from CComTearOffObjectBase and the interfaces you want your tear-off object to support.

Requirements

Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later
Header file: Declared in Atlcom.h
