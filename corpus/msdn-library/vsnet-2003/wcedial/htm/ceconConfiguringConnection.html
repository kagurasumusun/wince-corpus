Configuring the Connection

The RasCustomScriptExecute entry point can be invoked from RasDial. To invoke RasCustomScriptExecute from RasDial, the phone-book entry for the connection must specify the RASEO_CustomScript option.

For a description of phone-book entry options, see the dwfOptions member of RASENTRY.
