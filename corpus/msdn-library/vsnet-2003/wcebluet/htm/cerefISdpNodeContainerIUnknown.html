ISdpNodeContainer:IUnknown

This interface is used to manage the contents of a node.

Method Description
ISdpNodeContainer::AppendNode Appends NodeData at pData to the end of the container.
ISdpNodeContainer::CreateFromStream Creates a record from a stream.
ISdpNodeContainer::CreateStream Creates a stream from a record.
ISdpNodeContainer::GetNode Retrieves the contents of a node.
ISdpNodeContainer::GetNodeCount Retrieves the number of nodes in the Service Discovery Protocol (SDP) container.
ISdpNodeContainer::GetNodeStringData Retrieves the string data of a node.
ISdpNodeContainer::GetStreamSize Retrieves the size of the stream.
ISdpNodeContainer::GetType Retrieves the SDP container, sequence, or alternative type.
ISdpNodeContainer::LockContainer Locks or unlocks the container.
ISdpNodeContainer::SetNode Modifies the contents of a node.
ISdpNodeContainer::SetType Sets the SDP container, sequence, or alternative type.
ISdpNodeContainer::Walk Walks the SDP container and the containers within it.
ISdpNodeContainer::WriteStream Converts the contents of the container into a valid, well-formed raw SDP stream.
