Catalog Window

The Catalog window contains all of the Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET operating system configurations, board support packages, transport layers, reference hardware drivers, and run-time libraries. You can import additional features into the Catalog and add projects to your platform. For information about specific modules and features, see Catalog Features.

Use FeatureView and the Catalog together to view and modify your platform. FeatureView shows all of the objects currently in your platform; the Catalog shows all of the objects that you could add to your platform. Always move items from the Catalog to FeatureView rather than the other way around.

For information about the icons used in the Catalog window, see Object Icon Types.
