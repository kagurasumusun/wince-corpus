Creating and Managing a List of Favorite Help Topics

The Favorites tab allows you to create a list of topics that you reference often so you can easily find them when needed. You can also rename topics in the list and delete them from the list when they are no longer needed.

Note   You can add only individual topics to your Favorites tab.

To create and manage a list of favorite topics

  1. In the navigation pane, choose the Contents, Index, or Search tab, and then open a topic you want to add to your favorites list.
  2. Choose the Favorites tab.

    The topic that you opened in Step 1 will be displayed in the right pane.

  3. To add the topic to your favorites list, choose the Add button.
  4. If you want to rename the displayed topic, type a new name in the Current topic box, and then choose the Add button.
  5. To remove a topic from the list, select the topic, and then choose the Remove button.
  6. To display a topic in the list, select the topic, and then choose the Display button.
