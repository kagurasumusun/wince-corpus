How can you customize your new platform?

Once you have created your initial platform using the Platform Wizard, you can debug your configuration or continue to customize the platform by dragging the features from the Catalog into the FeatureView window. You can lock a feature into your platform by anchoring it, remove those without dependent features, or delete anchored features to shrink your platform size. Any feature you manually add to the platform will be anchored by default. You can also include your own projects, drivers, and transport layers by adding those projects to the platform.

For more information about customizing your platform, see Platform Customization.
