New Platform Wizard

The New Platform Wizard guides you through the process of creating a Windows CE–based platform.

The process of creating a new platform with the New Platform Wizard includes the following steps:

  1. Selecting an installed board support package (BSP).
  2. Selecting a platform configuration for your device.
  3. Selecting a configuration variant that is appropriate for your device.
  4. Selecting additional features for your platform.
