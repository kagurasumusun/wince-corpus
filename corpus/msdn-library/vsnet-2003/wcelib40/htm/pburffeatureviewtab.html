FeatureView Tab

In Platform view, the FeatureView tab shows all of the features that make up your platform. Once you have created a platform, you can modify it by adding and deleting features. You can create and add your own custom features. You can also add features from third-party vendors, and from the Catalog. The Catalog contains all of the operating system configurations, the reference hardware drivers, and run-time libraries.
