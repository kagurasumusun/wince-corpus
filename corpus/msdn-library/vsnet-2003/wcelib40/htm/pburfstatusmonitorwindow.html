Status Monitor Window

The Status Monitor window enables you to keep track of the state of the following services on your target device.

The following window describes the processes shown in the Status Monitor Window.

Process State
Target Control (CESH) Unknown, Stopped, Running, Waiting, or Timed Out
Target Messages (CETerm) Unknown, Stopped, Running, or Waiting
Debugger Unknown, Stopped, Running, Waiting, or Breaking
Download Unknown, Downloading, or Not Downloading
Data Visualization Unknown, Not Available, Running, Connected, or Not Connected
Build Unknown, Building, or Not Building
