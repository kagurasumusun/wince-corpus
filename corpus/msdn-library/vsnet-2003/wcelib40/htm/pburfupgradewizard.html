Upgrade Wizard

The Upgrade Wizard guides you through the steps of upgrading your Windows CE .NET 4.0–based platform to a Windows CE .NET 4.1–based platform.

To upgrade your existing platform

  • Select a new name and directory for your platform.

The wizard then presents you with the option to view the upgrade log files generated during the update.
