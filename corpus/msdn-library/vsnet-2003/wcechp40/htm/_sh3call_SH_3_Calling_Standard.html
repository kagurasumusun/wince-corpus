SH-3 Calling Sequence Specification

The SH-3 Calling Sequence Specification for Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET provides direction for the development of compilers and assembly language programs for SH-3 platforms. In addition, the standard enables the development of tools, debuggers, and operating system utilities that perform automated walking of the call stack.
