Questions About Submitting Feedback

Because the Microsoft® Windows® Embedded documentation team at Microsoft wants to know what you think about our documentation, we have provided a mechanism for submitting feedback by e-mail about the documentation. The following FAQ answers questions that you may have about submitting feedback and how your feedback will be used. If you are interested in submitting feedback on our documentation after reading this FAQ, please return to the Help topic you were reading and click Send Feedback at the bottom of the topic.

Q: Why are you collecting feedback?

A: Because we know our documentation can always be improved. Historically, contact between members of the documentation team and our customers has been limited to ad-hoc encounters at trade shows and conferences. We have found that our customers typically have very useful, meaningful, and thoughtful opinions about our documentation. We want more of that kind of feedback.

Q: What happens to my feedback after I send it?

A: We generally handle user feedback like this:

  1. Your feedback goes to an internal e-mail list monitored directly by members of the documentation team. We decide on a case-by-case basis who should handle the feedback. Feedback about technical content in the documentation (for example, errors, inconsistencies or oversights in the documentation instructions or examples) is reviewed by the writer who is responsible for the topics related to those issues. Feedback about terminology and overall content organization is reviewed by our editors. Feedback about broader, product-wide issues should be submitted to a more appropriate forum; we can only accept documentation-related feedback through this feedback channel. Feedback about non-documentation issues will be returned with suggestions for what other feedback channels would be more appropriate. See "What can I send feedback about?" for more information.
  2. A member of the documentation team will reply to your initial feedback as soon as possible, either to ask you for clarification about the issues you have raised or to inform you of what action we are taking in response to your feedback.
  3. When work items relating to your feedback have been completed, you will receive a final e-mail message informing you of what changes have been made to the documentation to address the issues you raised. Please note that there are many factors that affect whether we make changes based on feedback; although we take customer feedback very seriously, we may not be able to implement all suggestions we receive.

Q: What are you going to do with my e-mail address?

A: We keep track of your e-mail address only in order to communicate with you about the specific feedback you have sent. Your e-mail address will not be added to any mailing lists as a result of sending us feedback. If you are already on any mailing lists maintained by Microsoft, sending us feedback will not affect your membership in those lists. However, if you are not currently on any Windows Embedded mailing lists but you would like to be so that you can receive product announcements or other informational mailings from the Windows Embedded group, please let us know and we will forward your request to the Windows Embedded marketing team.

Q: What can I send feedback about?

A: Although we welcome your feedback on any issue, be aware that we created this feedback mechanism specifically for documentation issues. Issues such as bug reports, feature requests, and other product improvements should go through other channels. The following table shows the appropriate channels for different types of feedback and some brief guidelines for submitting feedback through those channels.

Bug reports If you are participating in a Beta test program for an EAPG product, please use the specific bug-reporting mechanisms provided through that program. Otherwise, use the Platform Builder or Windows CE newsgroups listed above, as appropriate for the bug you are reporting.

If you want to report a product bug, please be as specific as you can about the hardware and software you are using and the particular development scenario you are pursuing. We recognize and respect the fact that you may be under legal or business constraints regarding what you can discuss about your development scenarios. Under no circumstances should you reveal to us information about your development scenarios that is contrary to your company's policies or any third-party agreements applicable to your development efforts. We appreciate any detail you can provide.

Q: Why are you collecting feedback by e-mail instead of a Web-based form?

A: For several reasons. First, the linking functionality supported in HTML Help makes it possible for us to collect information about the specific product version you are using and which topic's feedback link you clicked. This is helpful information for us by itself, because it allows us to isolate problem areas in our documentation. Second, depending on the nature of your feedback, we may need to contact you for clarification on the issues you encountered. E-mail is a convenient mechanism for doing so. Third, e-mail is a free-form input mechanism; you can tell us as much as you choose about the product documentation. If we were to provide a Web-based questionnaire, we would have to anticipate your feedback in advance, which would artificially limit the kind of feedback we need the most--feedback we cannot anticipate.

Q: How is this feedback mechanism different from Microsoft Product Support Services, the MSDN Embedded Development Web site, and newsgroups?

A: This e-mail feedback mechanism is only intended for feedback on the Windows Embedded product documentation. Microsoft Product Support Services (PSS) offers many support options for our customers, including varying levels of technical support and a mechanism for feedback on Microsoft products. The MSDN®, the Microsoft Developer Network Embedded Development Web site and newsgroups provide you with convenient access to the worldwide community of developers working with Windows Embedded platforms; this community offers quick solutions to problems, discussion about programming and engineering best-practices for Windows Embedded platforms, and discussion with Microsoft staff working on Windows Embedded products. This e-mail feedback mechanism gives you a direct channel to the documentation team for comments you have about the documentation. By using these feedback channels, you can have a powerful voice in guiding our development efforts.

We appreciate the time you take to let us know what you think.
