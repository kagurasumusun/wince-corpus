MIPS NEC5432 Intrinsic Functions

The following table shows the MIPS intrinsic functions that support the NEC 5432 microprocessor.

Intrinsic function Corresponding MIPS NEC5432 instruction Description
_add_ob ADD.OB Adds the elements of two vectors, based on specified selection criteria.
_alni_ob ALNI.OB Performs a Vector Align of two vectors.
_and_ob AND.OB Combines the elements of two vectors in a bitwise logical AND operation, based on specified selection criteria
_c_eq_ob C.EQ.OB Compares the values in two vectors for equality, and writes the result to condition codes.
_c_le_ob C.LE.OB Compares the values in two vectors for less than or equal, writes the result to condition codes.
_c_lt_ob C.LT.OB Compares the values in two vectors for less than, writes the result to condition codes.
_max_ob MAX.OB Compares the values of two vectors, and writes the larger in a resultant vector.
_min_ob MIN.OB Compares the values of two vectors, and writes the smaller in a resultant vector.
_mul_ob MUL.OB Multiplies the elements of two vectors, based on specified selection criteria.
_mula_ob MULA.OB Multiplies the elements of two vectors, based on specified selection criteria, and adds the result to Accumulator.
_mull_ob MULL.OB Multiplies the elements of two vectors, based on specified selection criteria, and stores the result in the Accumulator.
_muls_ob MULS.OB Multiplies the elements of two vectors, based on specified selection criteria, and subtracts the result from the Accumulator.
_mulsl_ob MULSL.OB Multiplies the elements of two vectors, based on specified selection criteria, negates the product, and stores the result in the Accumulator.
_nor_ob NOR.OB Combines contents of two vectors in a bitwise logical NOR operation, based on specified selection criteria.
_or_ob OR.OB Combines the contents of two vectors in a bitwise logical OR operation, based on specified selection criteria.
_pickf_ob PICKF.OB Selects an element from one of two source vectors based on a specified condition. If the condition tests false, writes the selected element to a resultant vector.
_pickt_ob PICKT.OB Selects an element from one of two source vectors based on a specified condition. If the condition tests true, writes the selected element to a resultant vector.
_rach_ob RACH.OB Reads the most significant third bits of the Accumulator, and copies them into a resultant vector.
_racl_ob RACL.OB Reads the least significant third bits of the Accumulator, and copies them into a resultant vector.
_racm_ob RACM.OB Reads the middle third of the bits of the Accumulator, and copies them into a resultant vector.
_rzu_ob RZU.OB Logically shifts the values in the Accumulator to the right based on the values in a source vector.
_shfl_mixh_ob SHFL.MIXH.OB Performs a MIXH vector shuffle on two source vectors, and merges the elements into a resultant vector.
_shfl_mixl_ob SHFL.MIXl.OB Performs a MIXL vector shuffle on two source vectors, and merges the elements into a resultant vector.
_shfl_pach_ob SHFL.PACH.OB Performs a PACH vector shuffle on two source vectors, and merges the elements into a resultant vector.
_shfl_pacl_ob SHFL.PACL.OB Performs a PACL vector shuffle on two source vectors, and merges the elements into a resultant vector.
_sll_ob SLL.OB Shifts the elements of one source vector left by the amount specified in a second source vector.
_srl_ob SRL.OB Shifts the elements of one source vector right by the amount specified in a second source vector.
_sub_ob SUB.OB Subtracts the values of one vector from another vector, and writes the result into a third vector.
_wach_ob WACH.OB Writes the most significant third bits of the Accumulator from a specified vector.
_wacl_ob WACL.OB Writes the least significant two third bits of the Accumulator from a specified vector.
_xor_ob XOR.OB Combines the elements of two source vectors in a bitwise logical exclusive OR operation.
