SHASM Predefined .EQU Symbols

SHASM predefines the symbols _M_SH and _M_SH_REV as if they were .EQU symbols.

  • _M_SH is the SH version number
    • 1 for SH1
    • 3 for SH-3 or SH-3E
    • 4 for SH-4
  • _M_SH_REV is the revision number of the processor, in the range 0 through 99

If -Qsh4r1 is specified, _M_SH is 4, and _M_SH_REV is 1.
