About Supported Microprocessors

Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET supports numerous microprocessors through a variety of development tools, including compilers, intrinsic functions that allow full optimization, and assemblers for tasks that cannot be supported in other ways.

The following list shows the microprocessor families supported in Windows CE .NET:

  • ARM licensed technologies for architectures v4, v4T, Thumb, v5TE, and Intel XScale
  • Hitachi SuperH microprocessors SH-3, SH3-DSP, and SH-4
  • MIPS licensed technologies developed by NEC, Toshiba, Philips Semiconductor, Integrated Device Technologies, LSI Logic, and Quantum Effect Design
