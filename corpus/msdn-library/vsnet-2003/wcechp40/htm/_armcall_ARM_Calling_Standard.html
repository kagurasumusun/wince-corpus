ARM Calling Sequence Specification

The ARM Calling Sequence Specification for Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET provides information needed to use compilers and build assembly language programs for the ARM microprocessor. In addition, the standard enables the development of tools, debuggers, and operating system utilities that perform automated walking of the call stack.
