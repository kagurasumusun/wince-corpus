MIPS SandCraft SR1-GX Intrinsic Functions

This topic contains tables that show the MIPS intrinsic functions that support the SandCraft SR1-GX microprocessor. For easier reading, each table contains a subgroup of the functions in alphabetic order.

The following table shows MIPS intrinsic functions, _abs_sl through _add_ps.

Intrinsic function Corresponding MIPS SR1-GX instruction Description
_abs_sl ABS.fmt Calculates the absolute value of the lower half of a floating point double
_abs_su ABS.fmt Calculates the absolute value of the upper half of a floating point double
_abs_vl ABS.fmt Calculates the absolute value of the lower half of a vector value
_abs_vu ABS.fmt Calculates the absolute value of the upper half of a vector value
_abs_ps ABS.fmt Calculates the absolute value of the upper and lower halves of a paired single independently.
_add_sl ADD.fmt Adds the lower halves of two floating point numbers.
_add_su ADD.fmt Adds the upper halves of two floating point numbers.
_add_vl ADD.fmt Adds the lower halves of two vectors.
_add_vu ADD.fmt Adds the upper halves of two vectors.
_add_ps ADD.fmt Adds the upper and lower halves of two paired singles independently.

The following table shows MIPS intrinsic functions _cvt_ps_s through _mul_ps.

Intrinsic function Corresponding MIPS SR1-GX instruction Description
_cvt_ps_s cvt.l.fmt Converts two single precision floating-point values to a paired single value.
_cvt_s_pl cvt.s.fmt Converts the lower half of a paired single value to a single precision floating-point value.
_cvt_s_pu cvt.s.fmt Converts the upper half of a paired single value to a single precision floating-point value.
_div_sl DIV.fmt Divides the lower halves of two floating-point numbers
_div_su DIV.fmt Divides the upper halves of two floating-point numbers to produce a 64-bit result
_div_vl DIV.fmt Divide the lower halves of two vectors to produce a 64-bit result
_div_vu DIV.fmt Divide the upper halves of two vectors to produce a 64-bit result
_div_ps DIV.fmt Divide the halves of two paired singles independently to produce a 64-bit result.
_ersqrt_sl Not available Estimates the reciprocal square root of the lower half of a 64-bit input value, and stores the result.
_ersqrt_su Not available Estimates the reciprocal square root of the upper half of a 64-bit input value, and stores the result.
_ersqrt_ps Not available Estimates the reciprocal square root of the upper and lower halves of a 64-bit input value, and stores the result.
_ersqrt_vl Not available Estimates the reciprocal square root of the lower half of a 64-bit input vector, and stores the result.
_ersqrt_vu Not available Estimates the reciprocal square root of the upper half of a 64-bit input vector, and stores the result.
_madd_sl MADD.fmt Multiplies the lower halves of two numbers and adds the result to the Accumulator.
_madd_su MADD.fmt Multiplies the upper halves of two numbers and adds the result to the Accumulator.
_madd_vl MADD.fmt Multiplies the lower halves of two vectors and adds the result to the Accumulator.
_madd_vu MADD.fmt Multiplies the upper halves of two vectors and adds the result to the Accumulator.
_madd_ps MADD.fmt Multiply the upper and lower halves of two paired singles independently, and add the result to the Accumulator.
_mov_sl MOV.fmt Moves the lower half of a 64-bit number to a number.
_mov_su MOV.fmt Moves the upper half of a 64-bit number to a number.
_mov_vl MOV.fmt Moves the lower half of a 64-bit vector to a number.
_mov_vu MOV.fmt Moves the upper half of a 64-bit vector to a number.
_mov_ps MOV.fmt Moves the upper and lower halves of two paired singles independently.
_movf_ps MOVF Tests a paired single against a condition code. If floating point test is FALSE, moves value.
_movn_ps MOVN Tests a paired single against a condition code. If floating point tests not zero, moves value.
_movt_ps MOVT Tests a paired single against a condition code. If floating point test is TRUE, move value
_movz_ps MOVZ Tests a paired single against a condition code. If floating point tests zero, move value.
_msub_sl MSUB.fmt Multiplies the lower half of a double times a floating-point number, and subtracts the result from a third number.
_msub_su MSUB.fmt Multiplies the lower half of a double times a floating-point number, and subtracts the result from a third number.
_msub_ps MSUB.fmt Multiplies the upper and lower halves of two paired singles and subtracts the result from a third number.
_msub_vl MSUB.fmt Multiplies the lower halves of two vectors and subtracts the result from a third number.
_msub_vu MSUB.fmt Multiplies the upper halves of two vectors and subtracts the result from a third number.
_mul_sl MUL.fmt Multiplies the lower half of a double precision number times a single-precision number.
_mul_su MUL.fmt Multiplies the upper half of a double precision number times a single-precision number.
_mul_vl MUL.fmt Multiplies the lower halves of two vectors.
_mul_vu MUL.fmt Multiplies the upper halves of two vectors.
_mul_ps MUL.fmt Multiplies the upper and lower halves of two paired singles independently.
