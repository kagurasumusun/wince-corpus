Managing Connection Notification, File Converters, and Partnerships

The ActiveSync mechanism requires notification that the device has been connected to or disconnected from the desktop. This section explains how connection notification works, and the various methods of implementing it.

When files and data objects are transferred between the desktop and the device, their format is often converted to accommodate the reduced memory of the device. The conversion is performed by filters, whose use and construction is described in this section.

The relationship between a device and a desktop is called a partnership. This section explains how a partnership is set up and maintained.
