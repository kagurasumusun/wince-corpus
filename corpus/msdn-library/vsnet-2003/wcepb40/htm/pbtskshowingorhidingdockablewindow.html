Showing or Hiding a Dockable Window

  • To show a dockable window, choose the window name from the View menu.

    – or –

    To hide a dockable window, choose the Hide button in the window.

    You can show or hide the Output and Workspace windows at any time; however, you can show or hide debugging only windows during the debugging process.
