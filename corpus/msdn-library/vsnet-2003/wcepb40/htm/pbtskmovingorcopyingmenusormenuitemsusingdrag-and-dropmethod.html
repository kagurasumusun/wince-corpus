Moving or Copying Menus or Menu Items Using the Drag-and-Drop Method

  1. Drag or copy the item that you want to move to a new location on the current menu or to a different menu.

    You can navigate to another menu by dragging the cursor over it.

  2. Drop the menu item when the insertion guide shows the position you want.
