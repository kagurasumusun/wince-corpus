Graphics Editor Panes

The graphics editor typically displays a graphic in two panes separated by a split bar. One view is actual size and the other is enlarged. The default enlargement factor is 6. The views in these two panes are updated automatically: changes you make in one pane are immediately shown in the other.

The two panes make it easy for you to work on an enlarged view, in which you can distinguish individual pixels and, at the same time, observe the effect of your work on the actual-size graphic. If the graphic is 200 x 200 pixels or larger, however, only one pane is displayed initially. Move the split bar to display both panes. You can change the magnification in a pane using the Magnify tool and the magnification factor in the options selector.

You can use the two panes in the following ways:

  • To enlarge the smaller pane and use the two panes to show different area of a large graphic. Click in the pane to select it.
  • To change the relative sizes of the panes by positioning the cursor on the split bar and moving the split bar to the right or left.
  • To display a grid that delimits individual pixels, if the graphics editor pane is enlarged by a factor of four or greater.
