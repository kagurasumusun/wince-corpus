Selecting One or More Menu IGtems

  1. Select the menu or cascading menu you want.
  2. Select a menu item, or press the SHIFT key while selecting multiple menu items.

    – or –

    With the cursor outside the menu, drag the cursor to draw a selection box around the menu items to select.
