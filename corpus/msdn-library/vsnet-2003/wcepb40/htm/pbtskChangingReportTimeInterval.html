Changing the Report Time Interval

  1. From the Options menu, choose Report.
  2. In the Update Time area, choose Manual Update or Periodic Update.

    If you choose Periodic Update, in the Interval box type a number that specifies the time, in seconds, between updates to the report.

  3. Choose OK.
