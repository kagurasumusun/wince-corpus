Toggling the Transparency of the Background Color

  1. Select the appropriate button in the Graphics toolbar option selector.
  2. Choose Draw Opaque from the Image menu.

    Opaque background: The existing graphic is obscured by all parts of the selection.

    - or -

    Clear the Draw Opaque check box on the Image menu.

    Transparent background: The existing graphic shows through parts of the selection that match the current background color.
