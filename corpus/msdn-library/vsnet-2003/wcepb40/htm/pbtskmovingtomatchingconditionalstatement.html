Moving to a Matching Conditional Statement

  1. Move the insertion point to a line that is enclosed by preprocessor statements.
  2. Use the key combination you have chosen to invoke the ConditionalUp command to move the insertion point up to the line containing the matching preprocessor statement, such as #ifdef.
  3. Use the key combination you have chosen to invoke the ConditionalDown command to move the insertion point down to the line containing the matching preprocessor statement, such as #endif.
