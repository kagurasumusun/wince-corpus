Expanding or Contracting a Window in Docked Mode

  • Choose the Expand or Contract button.

    The Expand and Contract buttons are dual-purpose buttons. When the window is fully expanded, the function is Contract; otherwise, the function is Expand. The ToolTip and the button name indicate the current function.
