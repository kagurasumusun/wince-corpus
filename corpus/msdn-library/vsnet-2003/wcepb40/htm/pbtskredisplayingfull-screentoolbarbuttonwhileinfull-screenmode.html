Redisplaying the Full-Screen Toolbar Button While in Full-Screen Mode

  1. Press ALT+T to display the Tools menu.

    The menu bar is hidden in full-screen mode, but you can open a menu by using the keyboard.

  2. Choose Customize, and then choose the Toolbars tab.
  3. Select the Full-Screen check box, and then choose Close.
