Sizing a Group of Controls with Guides

  1. Snap one side of the control, or controls, to a guide.
  2. Drag a guide to the other side of the control, or controls.
  3. If necessary with multiple controls, size each to snap to the second guide.
  4. Move either guide to size the control, or controls, on that side.
