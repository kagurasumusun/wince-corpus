Stepping Out of a Function

  1. Start debugging and execute the application to some point inside a function.
  2. From the Debug menu, choose Step Out.

    - or -

    Choose the Step Out button on the Debug toolbar.

    The debugger continues until it has executed the return from the function, and then pauses.
