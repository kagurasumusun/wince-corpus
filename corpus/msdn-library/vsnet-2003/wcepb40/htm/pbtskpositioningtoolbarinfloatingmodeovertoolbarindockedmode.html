Positioning a Toolbar in Floating Mode over a Toolbar in Docked Mode

  1. Point to the title bar or to a blank area on the toolbar.
  2. Press CTRL, and then drag the toolbar over any docking area of the application window.

    The orientation of a toolbar in docked mode generally corresponds to the orientation of the docking area. Toolbars dock vertically on vertical docking areas and horizontally on horizontal docking areas.

  3. You can switch a toolbar's docked orientation between horizontal and vertical by pressing or releasing the SHIFT key as you drag the toolbar.
