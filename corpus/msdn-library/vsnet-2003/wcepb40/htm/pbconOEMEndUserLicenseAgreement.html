OEM End User License Agreement

Platform Builder automatically includes an end user license agreement, which is necessary to protect the intellectual property rights of Microsoft components. You have the option of adding your own license agreement. Platform Builder attaches an additional license agreement to the end of the Microsoft license agreement. Your license agreement must be saved as a text (.txt) file.
