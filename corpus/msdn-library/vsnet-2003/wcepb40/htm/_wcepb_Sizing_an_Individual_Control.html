Individual Control Sizing

Use the sizing handles to resize a control. When the cursor is positioned on a sizing handle, it changes shape to indicate the direction in which the control will be resized. If a sizing handle is hollow, indicating that it is inactive, the control cannot be resized along that axis.

You can also change the size of a control by snapping the control to guides or margins, or by moving one snapped control and guide away from another.
