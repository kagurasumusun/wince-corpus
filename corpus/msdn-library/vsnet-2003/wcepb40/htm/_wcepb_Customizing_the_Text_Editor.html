Text Editor Customization

You can set the text editor's behavior to suit your preferences and work habits in several areas, including how you want to save your files, what settings you want for tabs and indents, and what font style, size, and color you want.

You can customize the Edit toolbar, or place text editor commands on any menu or toolbar. For more information, see Toolbars and Menus.
