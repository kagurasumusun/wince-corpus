Microprocessor Register Access

eXDI provides access to a fixed set of microprocessor registers, corresponding to Platform Builder needs. To get more microprocessor registers or co-processor registers, you need additional interfaces or plug-ins.

Currently, Windows CE allows running on a single microprocessor at a time.

Although the Platform Builder debugger and the eXDI driver are designed accordingly, the driver can potentially support multiple microprocessors, even from different families, just not simultaneously. The driver should indicate which microprocessor family the currently active microprocessor belongs to in the TargetProcessorFamily field in GLOBAL_TARGET_INFO_STRUCT.

The CrONpxState member of CONTEXT_X86 in the IeXdiX86Context is not used and should be ignored. RegisterArea in the X86 context structure (IeXdiX86Context) contains the floating-point registers in the order ST0, ST1…ST7. Within each register, the less significant bytes appear first.
