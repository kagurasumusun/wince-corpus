Viewing a Dockable Window the Same Way As a Document Window

  1. Choose Options from the Tools menu, and then choose the Workspace tab.
  2. Select or clear the check box for the window in the Docking Views list to enable or disable its docking characteristics.

    By default, the Disassembly window has the characteristics of a document window.
