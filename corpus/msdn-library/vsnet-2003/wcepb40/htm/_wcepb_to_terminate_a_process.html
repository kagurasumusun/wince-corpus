Terminating a Process Using the Processes Window Toolbar

  • Choose the Terminate Process button on the Processes window toolbar.

    – or –

    Right-click a process in the Processes window, and then choose Terminate Process from the shortcut menu.

    The display in the Process window is refreshed, and the terminated process no longer appears in the window. Terminate Process is not available if the target is at a debug stop or if Target Control (CESH) is not running.
