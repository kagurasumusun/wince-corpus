Calculations for Raw Counter Data

To obtain information from a counter, you may need to convert the raw data generated by the counter into data that is more easily interpreted. Remote Performance Monitor supports the following counters, including calculations that can be used to convert raw data:

  • PERF_100NSEC_MULTI_TIMER
  • PERF_100NSEC_MULTI_TIMER_INV
  • PERF_100NSEC_TIMER
  • PERF_100NSEC_TIMER_INV
  • PERF_AVERAGE_BULK
  • PERF_AVERAGE_TIMER
  • PERF_COUNTER_BULK_COUNT
  • PERF_COUNTER_COUNTER
  • PERF_COUNTER_LARGE_RAWCOUNT
  • PERF_COUNTER_LARGE_RAWCOUNT_HEX
  • PERF_COUNTER_MULTI_TIMER
  • PERF_COUNTER_MULTI_TIMER_INV
  • PERF_COUNTER_QUEUELEN_TYPE
  • PERF_COUNTER_RAWCOUNT
  • PERF_COUNTER_RAWCOUNT_HEX
  • PERF_COUNTER_TIMER
  • PERF_COUNTER_TIMER_INV
  • PERF_ELAPSED_TIME
  • PERF_RAW_FRACTION
  • PERF_SAMPLE_COUNTER
  • PERF_SAMPLE_FRACTION
