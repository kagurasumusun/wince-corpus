Automatically Logging the Contents of the Modules and Symbols Window on Step

  • Right-click the list of modules in the Modules and Symbols window, and then choose Auto Log on Step from the shortcut menu.

    – or –

    Choose the Auto Log on Step button from the window toolbar.

    The information in the Modules and Symbols window is displayed on the Log tab of the Output window when the debugger takes a step.
