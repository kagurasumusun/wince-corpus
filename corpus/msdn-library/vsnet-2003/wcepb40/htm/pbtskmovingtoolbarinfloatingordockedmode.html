Moving a Toolbar in Floating or Docked Mode

  1. Point to a blank area on the toolbar for a toolbar in docked mode or to the title bar for a toolbar in floating mode.
  2. Drag the toolbar to the new location.

    If you drag the toolbar to the edge of the application window, it will dock to the edge of the window.
