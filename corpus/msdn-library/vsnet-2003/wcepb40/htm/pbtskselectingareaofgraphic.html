Selecting an Area of a Graphic

  1. Choose a selection tool in the Graphics toolbar.
  2. Move the insertion point to one corner of the graphic area to select it.

    Cross hairs appear when the insertion point is over the graphic.

  3. Drag the insertion point to the opposite corner of the area to select.

    A rectangle shows which pixels will be selected. All pixels within the rectangle, including those under the rectangle, are included in the selection.

  4. Release the mouse button.

    The selection border encloses the selected area. Any operation you perform will affect only the pixels within the rectangle.
