Moving or Copying Menus or Menu Items Using the Menu Commands

  1. Select one or more menus or menu items.
  2. Choose Cut to remove a menu or menu item from the Edit menu.

    – or –

    Choose Copy to copy a menu item that you want to move to another location.

    If you are moving the items to another menu or .rc file, open that menu editor window.

  3. Select the position of the menu or menu item you want to move or copy to.
  4. Choose Paste from the Edit menu.

    The moved or copied item is placed before the item that you select.

You can also drag, copy, and paste to other menus in other menu windows.
