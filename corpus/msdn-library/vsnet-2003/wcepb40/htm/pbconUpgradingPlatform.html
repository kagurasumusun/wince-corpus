Upgrading a Platform

When you open a Platform Builder 4.0 platform, the Platform Upgrade Wizard guides you through the process of upgrading the Platform Builder 4.0 platform to Platform Builder 4.1.

Note    The Platform Upgrade Wizard will not include third-party components in the converted platform.

To upgrade a platform

  1. From the File menu, choose Open Platform, select the platform you want to open, and then choose Open.
  2. Choose Yes to confirm that you want to upgrade your platform to Platform Builder 4.1.
  3. When the Platform Upgrade Wizard appears, choose Next.

    If you have installed Platform Builder 4.1 side-by-side with Platform Builder 4.0, a dialog box appears requesting a new name and location for your platform. If you have not performed a side-by-side installation, proceed to step 5.

  4. In the Name and Location fields, enter a new name and location path, and then choose Next.
  5. In the congratulations dialog box, choose Finish to save the converted platform and open it in Platform Builder.
