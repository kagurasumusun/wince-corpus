Jumping to an OS Image on the Target Device

  1. From the Target menu, choose Configure Remote Connection.
  2. Choose the Settings tab.
  3. Clear the Download Image check box.
  4. Choose OK.
  5. From the Target menu, choose Download/Initialize.
