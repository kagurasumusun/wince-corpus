Setting a Breakpoint at the Beginning of a Function

  1. Enter the function name in the Find box on the Standard toolbar.
  2. Choose the Insert/Remove Breakpoint button on the Build toolbar.

    A red dot appears in the left margin of your source code at the beginning of the function, indicating that the breakpoint is set.
