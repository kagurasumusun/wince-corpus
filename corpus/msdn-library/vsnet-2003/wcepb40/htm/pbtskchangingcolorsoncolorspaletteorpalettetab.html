Changing Colors on the Colors Palette or Palette Tab

  1. Choose Adjust Colors from the Image menu.
  2. Define the color by typing RGB or hue, saturation, and luminosity (HSL) values in the appropriate text boxes.
  3. Set the luminance by moving the slider on the luminance bar.

    Luminance is the perceived brightness of a given color, as opposed to its hue or its saturation.

  4. Many custom colors are dithered. If you want the solid color closest to the dithered color, double-click the Color preview window. If you later decide you want the dithered color, move the slider or the cross hairs again to restore dithering.
  5. Choose OK to add the new color.
