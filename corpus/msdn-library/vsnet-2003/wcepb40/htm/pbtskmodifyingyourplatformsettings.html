Modifying Your Platform Settings

  1. Choose the FeatureView tab on the Workspace window.
  2. Choose a feature or platform, right-click, and then choose Settings.

    - or -

    Choose Settings from the Platform menu.

    The General, Build Options, Locale and Environment tabs are displayed when you select a platform. If you select a feature, the General, Custom Build and Bib Info tabs are displayed.

  3. Choose the applicable configuration in the Settings For box.
  4. Choose from the tabs in the Platform Settings dialog box to view and modify items, and then choose OK.
