Displaying the Properties of an Event

  • In the system pane, double-click the icon corresponding to the event.

    - or -

    In the system pane, move the pointer over the icon corresponding to the event.

    - or -

    In the system pane, click once on the icon corresponding to the event, and then from the View menu, choose Event Properties.
