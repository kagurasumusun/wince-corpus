Graphic Selection and Editing

The selection tools define an area of a graphic that you can cut, copy, clear, resize, invert colors, or move. You can also create a custom brush from the selection.

You can perform standard editing operations — cutting, copying, clearing, and moving — with the selection, whether the selection is the entire graphic or just a part of it. Because the graphics editor uses the Clipboard, you can transfer graphics between the graphics editor and other Windows applications, such as Microsoft® Word.

In addition, you can resize the selection, whether it includes the entire graphic or just a part of it.
