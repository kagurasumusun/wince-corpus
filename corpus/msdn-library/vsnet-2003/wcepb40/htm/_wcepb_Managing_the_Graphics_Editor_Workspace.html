Graphics Editor Workspace

By adjusting the graphics editor workspace to fit your preferences, you can work more effectively.

By default, the graphics editor displays the view in the left pane at its actual size and the right pane view at a magnification factor of 6. The magnification factor is the ratio between the actual graphic size and the displayed size. The default is 6, and the range is from 1 through 8.
