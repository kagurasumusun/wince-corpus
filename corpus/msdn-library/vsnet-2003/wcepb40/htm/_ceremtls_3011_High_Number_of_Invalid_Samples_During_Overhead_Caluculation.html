Error 3011: High Number of Invalid Samples During Overhead Calculation

An unusually high number of invalid samples were detected during Overhead Calculation. 
Please check the P6IgnoreCounterOverride setting in the IceCAP.ini file, 
or contact Remote Call Profiler support.

This error is most likely caused by a user-defined counter generating a transition event.
