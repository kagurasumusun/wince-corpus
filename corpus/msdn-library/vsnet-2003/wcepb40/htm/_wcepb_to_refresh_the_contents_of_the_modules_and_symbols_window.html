Refreshing the contents of the Modules and Symbols window

  • Right-click the list of modules in the Modules and Symbols window, and then choose Refresh on the shortcut menu.

    – or –

    Choose the Refresh button on the window toolbar.

    If the data in the Modules and Symbols window is fresh, the Refresh button is disabled. If the data is outdated, the Refresh button is enabled.

    Note   Refresh does not load or unload symbols.
