Recording a Quick Macro

  1. Choose Record Quick Macro from the Tools menu.
  2. Perform the actions to record the macro.
  3. Use the Pause button on the Macro Recorder toolbar as a toggle to temporarily interrupt and resume recording.
  4. Choose the Stop button on the Macro Recorder toolbar when you have finished.
  5. Choose Play Quick Macro from the Tools menu to replay the macro.

This temporary macro remains available until you record another quick macro, which writes over any existing quick macro.
