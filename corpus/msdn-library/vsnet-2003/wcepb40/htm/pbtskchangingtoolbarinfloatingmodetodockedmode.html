Changing a Toolbar in Floating Mode to Docked Mode

  1. Point to the title bar or to a blank area on the toolbar.
  2. Drag the toolbar to any of the four borders of the application window.

    When you reach the boundary of a docking area with the pointer, the toolbar assumes a shape that is appropriate for the docking location. Along the top and bottom borders, it becomes a single horizontal row of buttons; along the right or left borders, it becomes a single vertical row.

    – or –

    Double-click the title bar to move the toolbar in floating mode to its last position in docked mode.

    If the toolbar has never been docked, it moves to a new row in the toolbar dock below the menu bar.
