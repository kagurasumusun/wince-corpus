Resizing an Entire Graphic Using Sizing Handles

  1. Select the entire graphic.
  2. If part of the graphic is currently selected, and you want to select the entire graphic, click anywhere on the graphic outside the current selection border.
  3. Drag any sizing handle until the graphic is the size you want.

    When you increase the graphic's size, the graphics editor extends the graphic to the right, down, or both, and fills the new area with the current background color. The graphic is not stretched.

    When you decrease the graphic's size, the graphics editor crops the graphic on the right or bottom edge, or both. The cropped portion of the graphic is permanently lost.
