Adjusting the Spacing Between Controls

  1. Select the controls to align.
  2. Choose Space Evenly from the Layout menu, and then choose one of the following alignment options:
    • Across, which spaces controls evenly between the leftmost and the rightmost control selected.
    • Down, which spaces controls evenly between the topmost and the bottommost control selected.
