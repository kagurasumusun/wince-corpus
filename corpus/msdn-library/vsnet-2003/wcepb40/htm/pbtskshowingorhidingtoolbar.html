Showing or Hiding a Toolbar

  1. Choose Customize from the Tools menu, and then choose the Toolbars tab.
  2. Select the check box for the toolbar to display it.

    – or –

    Clear the check box for the toolbar to hide it in the Toolbars box.

You can hide the menu bar only when all toolbars are hidden automatically. The menu bar is hidden automatically when you begin editing in full-screen mode.
