Cesh.exe Registry Entries

Setup.exe creates the registry key settings for Cesh.exe. The settings are located in the subkey HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\ppsh\default. Use Regedit.exe to view these settings.

The new Ppshell.sys driver uses the settings in the development workstation OS for low-level parameters such as InterruptVector, InterruptLevel, and PortAddress instead of reading these settings from the ppsh subkey.

Some target devices may require nondefault registry key settings. To provide custom support for a specific target device, register a unique profile under the ppsh\profile subkey. For example, target device settings may be registered under the \ppsh\MY_CEPC subkey.
