Creating, Setting, and Deleting a Guide

  1. Click anywhere within the rulers to create a guide.
  2. Drag the guide into position.

    The number of dialog units is displayed in the ruler and below on the status bar. After you have dragged the guide into position, hold the cursor over the guide arrow in the ruler to see the exact guide position.

  3. To delete a guide, drag the guide out of the dialog box.
