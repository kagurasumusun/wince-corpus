Pasting the Clipboard Contents into a Graphic

  1. Choose Paste from the Edit menu.

    The Clipboard contents, surrounded by the selection border, appear in the upper-left corner of the pane.

  2. Position the cursor within the selection border and drag the graphic to the location you want on the graphic.
  3. Click outside of the selection border to anchor the graphic at its new location.
