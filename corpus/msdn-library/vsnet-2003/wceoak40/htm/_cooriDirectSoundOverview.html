DirectSound Overview

The DirectSound feature for Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET is the wave-audio component of Microsoft DirectX®. Microsoft DirectSound® provides low-latency mixing, hardware acceleration, and direct access to the sound device. It provides this functionality while maintaining compatibility with existing device drivers.

DirectSound enables wave sound capture and playback. It also supports property sets, which allows you to take advantage of extended services offered by sound cards and their associated drivers.
