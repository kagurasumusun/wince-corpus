Waveform Audio Overview

The Waveform Audio feature for Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET provides all of the capabilities that are necessary for playing and recording waveform audio. The application programming interface (API) for this feature gives an application exact control over waveform audio input/output (I/O) devices.

The Waveform Audio feature also includes a hardware audio mixer that controls the routing of audio lines to a destination device for playing or recording. It can also control volume and other effects.
