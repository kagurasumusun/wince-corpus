Digital Rights Management Security

Security issues specific to DRM are provided with the unique digital signed components that you must obtain from Microsoft to build DRM enabled platforms. For information about how to obtain DRM components for Windows CE, see Digital Rights Management Implementation Considerations.

Adding support for the DRM license acquisition OCX to your platform will also add Internet Explorer 5.5 to your platform. For more information, see Internet Explorer 5.5 Security.

The successful and continued operation of DRM on your platform depends on having DRM certificates stored in a secure manner. You should ensure that the DRM files on your platform remain hidden in your ROM image and are never copied to RAM. In addition, Microsoft recommends that you take whatever additional steps you can, such as file encryption, code obfuscation, and implementation of hardware design features, to protect the DRM files in your ROM image.

It is in your interest to protect the DRM certificates on your platform. If these certificates are compromised on any of your DRM client devices then it may be necessary for Microsoft to revoke those certificates at the DRM license servers. This will disable DRM on those client devices and prevent them from accessing any content protected with DRM. Because of this, the hardware design of your Windows CE device should allow for flash updates. This is the only way to restore DRM support to a device whose DRM certificates have been revoked.
