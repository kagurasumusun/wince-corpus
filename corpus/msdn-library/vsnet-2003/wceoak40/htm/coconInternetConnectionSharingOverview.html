Internet Connection Sharing Overview

The Internet Connection Sharing feature for Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET consists of a collection of technologies and services that make it possible to connect multiple computing and information devices on a network located in a home, a small business, or a corporate branch office to the Internet through a single Internet connection. Computing resources on a home network include personal computers, video cameras, printers, scanners, and many other computing or home automation devices.
