Printing Overview

The Printing feature for Microsoft® Windows® CE .NET supports the ability to send printing commands to device drivers. Printing is supported over parallel port, universal serial bus (USB), serial port, and Bluetooth wireless technology. For information about configuring printing support, see Printer Drivers.
