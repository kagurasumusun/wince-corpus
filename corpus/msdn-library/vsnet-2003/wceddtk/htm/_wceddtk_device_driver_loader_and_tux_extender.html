Device Driver Loader and Tux Extender

The Device Driver Loader and Tux Extender (DDLX), Ddlx.dll, is a set of tools that extends the capabilities of the Tux Test Harness to allow your test DLL to operate in either an application or a device.exe address space.

When using Tux alone, your test DLL is loaded into Tux's address space. However, when using DDLX in combination with Tux, Ddlx.dll is loaded into Tux's address space instead. Ddlx.dll then registers your test DLL as a device driver in the Device.exe address space and provides an interface to your test DLL. This allows your test DLL to run in the Device.exe address space while still taking advantage of Tux's features.

Once your test DLL is registered, Ddlx.dll, Drvioctl.cpp, and Drvioctl.h work in unison to translate all ShellProc and TestProc messages from Tux to DeviceIoControl calls, which can then be handled by your test DLL. As your test DLL executes and returns back to DDLX, DDLX will accept the return codes from your test DLL and translate them into valid Tux return codes. DDLX provides a way for Tux and your test DLL to transparently communicate across these different address spaces.
