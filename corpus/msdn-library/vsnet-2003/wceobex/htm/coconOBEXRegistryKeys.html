OBEX Registry Settings

The registry stores information necessary to configure the system for applications and hardware devices. The registry also contains information that the operating system (OS) continually references during operation.

Enables OBEX IrDA hit bit

[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Comm\IrDA]
    "Hints"=dword:8224

Enable the OBEX Server

[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Services\OBEX]
    "DLL"="OBEXSrVr.dll"
    "Keep"=dword:1
    "Order"=dword:9
    "Prefix"="OBX"
    "Index"=dword:0

IrDA and Bluetooth Transports

[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Software\Microsoft\Obex\Transports\{30a7bc02-59b6-40bb-aa2b-89eb49ef274e}]
    "Name"="IRDA"
[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Software\Microsoft\Obex\Transports\{30a7bc03-59b6-40bb-aa2b-89eb49ef274e}]
