D3dCreateSurfaceEx Handles and DirectDraw DDIs

Handles do not completely insulate a DirectX driver from the DirectDraw-managed DDRAWI_DDSURFACE_MORE and DDRAWI_DDSURFACE_LCL structures. These structure names are essentially aliases for the structures DDRAWI_DDRAWSURFACE_MORE and DDRAWI_DDRAWSURFACE_LCL. In DirectDraw DDIs, such as Blt, Flip, the driver will still be passed surface structure pointers, and still must be able to use these structures instead of its private representations.
