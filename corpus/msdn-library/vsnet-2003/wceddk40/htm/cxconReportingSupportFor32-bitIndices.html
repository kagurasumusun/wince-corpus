Reporting Support for 32-bit Indices

Prior to DirectX 8.0 vertex indices were restricted to 16-bit quantities. DirectX 8.0 adds support for 32-bit indices. A driver reports support for 32-bit indices by setting the value of the MaxVertexIndex field of D3DCAPS8 (currently also in D3DHAL_D3DEXTENDEDCAPS) to a value greater than 0xFFFF. This field also allows the driver to report that although it supports indices requiring 32-bits of storage it does not support the full range of 32-bit values.
