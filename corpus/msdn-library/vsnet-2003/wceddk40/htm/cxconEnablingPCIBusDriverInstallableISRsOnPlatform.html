PCI Device Support

The PCI bus is a well-understood technology that is gaining traction in some embedded device categories. All modern desktop computers have PCI expansion slots for peripheral devices; these replace the ISA slots used in the past. In embedded systems, PCI devices can also be embedded into system motherboards. Support for PCI bus makes it possible to include a wide variety of inexpensive PCI devices in Windows CE–based platforms.

Like PCMCIA, PCI has Plug and Play and power management features. Unlike our current implementation of PCMCIA, properly written PCI device drivers can be bus agnostic, which means, the driver does not need to know that it is running on a PCI bus. As a result, the same device driver can be used in ISA, PCI, and direct system bus designs.
