Vertex Blending

Vertex blending operations are supported. Vertex blending works in this way: an object from modeling space is multiplied by a 4X4 world matrix, placing the model's origin in a particular world space, relative to the origin of that world space. One part of the matrix does orientation and another part does the position. There can be up to three world matrices applied, allowing objects to be bent by blending the vertices with different weighting over the span of the object. A sample can be found in the desktop DirectX 8.0 SDK.

Next, the view matrix is applied, which effectively compresses the space relative to a particular viewpoint. Much like a camera, this compresses the real world onto a two-dimensional picture.
