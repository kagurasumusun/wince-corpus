ATI Sample Display Driver

The ATI sample display driver supports a mixture of software and hardware acceleration, but only works with the Victoria PCI Graphics Development Board, a device that uses the ATI Rage XL graphics controller chip set. The ATI sample driver is only a GPE-based driver. In addition, the ATI sample display driver supports 8, 16, 24 and 32 bit-per-pixel color depths. This driver has only been tested at a resolution of 800x600 and a color depth of 16 bits.

The ATI sample driver supports the modes in the following table.

Horizontal resolution Vertical resolution Color depth in bits
640 480 8
640 480 16
640 480 32
720 480 16
720 480 32
800 600 8
800 600 16
800 600 32
1024 768 8
1024 768 16
1024 768 32
1280 1024 8
1280 1024 16
1280 1024 32
