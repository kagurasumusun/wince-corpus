Troubleshooting a Device Driver

In the process of developing your device driver, you may encounter problems building, loading, debugging, or downloading an OS image for your driver.

The following table shows common driver development problems and their possible causes.

Problem Possible cause
You cannot build your driver. May relate to compiling, linking, or the build environment. For information on how to correct this problem, see Troubleshooting: Building a Driver.
You cannot download your OS image. May relate to the connection to your target device or your hardware configuration. For information on how to correct this problem, see Troubleshooting: Downloading an OS Image.
You cannot load your driver. May relate to the registry. For information on how to correct this problem, see Troubleshooting: Loading a Driver.
You cannot debug your driver. May relate to setting breakpoints or debug zones. For information on how to correct this problem, see Troubleshooting: Debugging a Driver.
