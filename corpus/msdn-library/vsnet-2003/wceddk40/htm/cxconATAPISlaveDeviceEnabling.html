ATAPI Slave Device Enabling

In your Platform.reg file, the ALI, Intel, and Generic ATAPI/IDE controller registries are comments. If you have a master and slave configuration, uncomment these registry entries.

Note   If you uncomment these registry entries, you may experience delays in booting your system.
