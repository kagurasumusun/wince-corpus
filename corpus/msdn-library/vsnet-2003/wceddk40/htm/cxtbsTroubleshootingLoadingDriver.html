Troubleshooting: Loading a Driver

The following table shows problems you may encounter in the process of loading your driver.

Problem Possible solution
Your driver will not load. Verify the registry keys under the HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE registry subtree.
You are developing a PC Card driver, you have verified the settings under the HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE registry subtree, and your driver will not load. Step through the PC Card source code and verify that the Plug and Play identifier read correctly. Because Windows CE is an embedded, real-time OS, when the PC Card signals that it is ready, there is not as much time for initialization as a PC Card on a Windows-based desktop platform. On Windows CE, if the PC Card signals that it is ready for initialization, the OS assumes that it is in fact ready. The initialization time varies from one PC Card to another. On a Windows-based desktop platform, this is not a problem because there is extra time allowed for initialization. On Windows CE, this lack of extra time can prevent the OS from initializing the correct driver.
Your driver loads correctly, and then immediately unloads. Ensure that your DriverEntry and DllMain functions are exported in the .def file for your driver.
