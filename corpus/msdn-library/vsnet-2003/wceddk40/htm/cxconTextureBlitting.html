Texture Blitting

An important thing to notice is that textures are blitted - not by a direct call to Blt - but by embedding a token in the D3dDrawPrimitives2 command stream. This new token is D3DDP2OP_TEXBLT, and it signals the driver that a texture has to be transferred from a backing surface into local or nonlocal video memory.
