Audio Mixer

The audio mixer architecture provides routing services to manage audio lines on a device. The routing services can be used if you have adequate hardware devices and software drivers installed. The mixer architecture allows several audio source lines to map to a single destination audio line.

The audio mixer driver is responsible for ensuring that the correct destination device receives the audio source lines for playing or recording.
