PC Card Power Example

There is a way to remove power from a PC Card and powering it back up requires you to remove, insert, suspend, or resume.

Under the HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Drivers\Active registry key, there is a numbered key with the values of Sckt, PnpId, Hnd, and Name. These values are defined in %_WINCEROOT%\Public\Common\DDK\INC\Devload.h. Because Sckt and PnpId exist under this key, it is a PCMCIA or CF device. The first three characters of Name are DSK. The DWORD value in Hnd is used in a call to DeactivateDevice. Then, the disk driver frees its PCMCIA resources, which causes PCMCIA to power off the slot that the card was in.
