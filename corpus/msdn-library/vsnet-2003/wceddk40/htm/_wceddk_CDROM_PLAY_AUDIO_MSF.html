CDROM_PLAY_AUDIO_MSF

This structure contains information on the starting and ending minutes, seconds, and frames of the track.

typedef struct _CDROM_PLAY_AUDIO_MSF {
UCHAR StartingM; 
UCHAR StartingS; 
UCHAR StartingF; 
UCHAR EndingM; 
UCHAR EndingS; 
UCHAR EndingF; 
} CDROM_PLAY_AUDIO_MSF, *PCDROM_PLAY_AUDIO_MSF;

Members

StartingM
Starting track minutes number.
StartingS
Starting track seconds number.
StartingF
Starting track frame number.
EndingM
Ending track minutes number.
EndingS
Ending track seconds number.
EndingF
Ending track frame number.
