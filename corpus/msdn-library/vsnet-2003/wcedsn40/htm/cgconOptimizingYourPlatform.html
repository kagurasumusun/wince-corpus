Optimizing Your Platform

To verify that you can boot your operating system (OS) on your device, even if your goal is to build a more advanced configuration, you should begin by building and booting a minimal configuration. Because of the small number of components involved, it will take less time for you to design, test, and build an image. For more information, see Minimal Configurations Implementation Options.

To measure operating system performance on your targeted platform, you can create profiling images. This will enable you to determine where optimizing code would provide the most benefit. For more information about profiling techniques, see Platform Profiling.

Refer to the following topics for specific information about optimizing your platform:

  • Using RAM, ROM, and Mass Storage
