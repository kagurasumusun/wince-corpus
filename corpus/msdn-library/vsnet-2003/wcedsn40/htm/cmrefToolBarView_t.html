ToolBarView_t

This class provides methods for drawing a toolbar.

Method Description
ToolBarView_t::CalcOffset Determines the offset at which the button image on a toolbar is drawn when the button is pressed or in a checked state.
ToolBarView_t::DrawButtonBorder Draws the border of a button on a toolbar.
ToolBarView_t::DrawDropdownArrow Draws a drop-down arrow on a toolbar button.
ToolBarView_t::FaceRect Retrieves the bounding rectangle of the face of a button.
ToolBarView_t::InitializeButtonSlopValue Retrieves the amount of horizontal and vertical space that the border and sides of a button occupy, including a margin of one pixel.
ToolBarView_t::LoadSystemBitmap Loads the system bitmap that the toolbar requires.
