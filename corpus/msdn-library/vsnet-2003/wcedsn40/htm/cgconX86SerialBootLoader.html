x86 Serial Boot Loader

The x86 serial boot loader is modeled after Eboot.bin but uses the COM1 UART for transferring data to the development workstation. The sources for the serial boot loader are located in the %_WINCEROOT%\Platform\Cepc\Sboot directory.
