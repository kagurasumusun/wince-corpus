Implementing Support for OSBench

OSBench.exe is used to measure the performance of the scheduler. The measured performance is stored in two areas: %_WINCEROOT%\Public\Common\Oak\Utils\Osbench and %_WINCEROOT%\Public\Common\Oak\Utils\Ob_load. OSBench.exe works by using system resources to track the amount of time required to perform some scheduling tasks. The only requirement that OSBench.exe has on the OEM is to support the QueryPerformanceCounter and QueryPerformanceFrequency.
