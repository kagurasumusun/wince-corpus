Enhancing OAL Functionality

In this step, you are responsible for implementing the features you choose to include in your OAL.

To enhance OAL functionality

  1. Implement any additional ISRs.
  2. Manage the clock and timers.
  3. Configure the debugging options.
  4. Enable power management.
  5. Provide platform information for applications.
  6. Manage event tracking.
