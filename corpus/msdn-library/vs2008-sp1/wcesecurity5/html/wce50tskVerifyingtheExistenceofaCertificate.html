Verifying the Existence of a Certificate

After enrolling for a certificate, you can verify the certificate from Control Panel on your device.

To verify the certificate

  1. From the Start menu of the CEPC device, point to Settings, and then choose Control Panel.

    - or -

    From the Start menu of the Emulator on your development workstation, point to Settings, and then choose Control Panel.

  2. In Control Panel, double-click Certificates.

    The Certificates dialog box opens.

  3. From the Stores drop down box, choose My Certificates.

    You will see your user certificate.
