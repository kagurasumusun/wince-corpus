Building and Downloading a Run-Time Image

After you create and customize an OS design in Platform Builder, you can build the run-time image and download it to a target device that is attached to your development workstation.

You can select tasks to perform, based on what you want the completed run-time image to look like and the configuration of your target device. For example, you can build selected Catalog items in your OS design so that you do not have to rebuild the entire run-time image again.
