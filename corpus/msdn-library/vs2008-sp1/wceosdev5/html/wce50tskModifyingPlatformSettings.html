Modifying OS Design Settings

You can modify the settings for your OS design in the Platform Builder IDE.

To modify platform settings

  1. In the Workspace window, choose the OSDesignView tab.
  2. Right-click the Catalog item or OS design and choose Settings.

    - or -

    From the Platform menu, choose Settings.

  3. In the Configuration box, choose the applicable configuration.

    For more information about configurations, see Levels of Debugging Support.

  4. In the Platform Settings dialog box, choose from the tabs to view and modify items; then choose OK to save changes and close the dialog box.
