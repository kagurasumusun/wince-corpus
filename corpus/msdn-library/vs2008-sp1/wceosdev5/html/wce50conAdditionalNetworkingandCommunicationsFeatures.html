Additional Networking and Communications Catalog Items

In most cases, the New Platform Wizard provides you with a list of additional networking and communications Catalog items that are available for the OS design you are creating. The inclusion of this list of Catalog items depends on the design template you based your OS design on.

The Catalog items selected by default are based on the OS design template you chose. These defaults are a starting point, not mandatory Catalog items and settings for your OS design.

You can add or remove the networking and communications Catalog items by checking or clearing them in the New Platform Wizard, or by manually making these changes in the Catalog.
