OEM End User License Agreement

When you develop an SDK, Platform Builder includes an end user license agreement (EULA), which is necessary to protect the intellectual property rights of Microsoft components.

You can add your own EULA. Before adding the your EULA to the SDK, you must save the EULA as a Rich Text Format (.rtf) file.

Your EULA appears separately at installation time, and the user must accept the terms and conditions of both EULAs before installing and using your SDK.
