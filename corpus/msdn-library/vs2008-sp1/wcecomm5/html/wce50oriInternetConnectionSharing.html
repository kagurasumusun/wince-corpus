Internet Connection Sharing

Internet Connection Sharing (ICS) for Microsoft Windows CE consists of a collection of technologies and services that make it possible to connect multiple computing and information devices on a network located in a home, a small business, or a corporate branch office to the Internet through a single Internet connection.
