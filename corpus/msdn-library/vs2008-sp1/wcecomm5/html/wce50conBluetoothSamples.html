Bluetooth Samples

The samples provide a base for implementing the Bluetooth technology. These samples have not been tested and are not intended for production use.

In this Section

Bluetooth OS Design Development Samples
Describes the samples in the %_WINCEROOT%\Public\Common\Oak\Drivers\Bluetooth\Sample directory, that provide a base for implementing the Bluetooth technology in your OS design.
Bluetooth Application Development Samples
Describes the samples in the %_WINCEROOT%\Public\Common\Sdk\Samples\Bluetooth directory, that provide a base for implementing the Bluetooth technology in your Windows CE-based applications.
