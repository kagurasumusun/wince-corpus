Home and Today Screen Guidelines

Send Feedback on this topic to the authors

On Windows Mobile-based devices, the Home screen and Today screen provide users with a starting page designed to show dynamic, relevant information at a glance.

The screens include components that a user clicks to open applications. In designing your application, consider what information the user might want instant access to and display this information when the component is selected. Include secondary information in the application setting screen or other application screens.
