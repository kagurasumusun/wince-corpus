VR_MSG

Send Feedback on this topic to the authors

This enumeration contains messages generated by the SendMessage function to the Voice Recorder Control application. None of the messages take arguments from the wparam or lparam parameters.

typedef enum tagVR_MSG {
  VRM_RECORD,
  VRM_PLAY,
  VRM_STOP,
  VRM_CANCEL,
  VRM_OK,
} VR_MSG;

Enumerators

VRM_RECORD
Starts recording.
VRM_PLAY
Plays the recording.
VRM_STOP
Stops recording or playing.
VRM_CANCEL
Cancels, closes, and discards any recording in progress.
VRM_OK
Closes and saves a recording.

Requirements

Smartphone Platforms: Smartphone 2002 and later
Pocket PC Platforms: Pocket PC 2000 and later
OS Versions: Windows CE 3.0 and later
Header: voicectl.h
Library: voicectl.lib
