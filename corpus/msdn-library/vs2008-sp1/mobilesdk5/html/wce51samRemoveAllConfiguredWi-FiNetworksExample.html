Remove All Configured Wi-Fi Networks Example

Send Feedback on this topic to the authors

The following example removes all configured networks from the device, regardless of their SSID or network type.

<wap-provisioningdoc>
    <nocharacteristic type="Wi-Fi"/>
</wap-provisioningdoc>
