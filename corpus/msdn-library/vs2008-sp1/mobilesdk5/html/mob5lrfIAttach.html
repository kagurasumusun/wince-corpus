IAttach

Send Feedback on this topic to the authors

The IAttach interface is used to maintain and provide access to the properties of attachments in messages.

Note   IAttach has no unique methods of its own.

Methods in Vtable Order

  • IUnknown methods
  • IMAPIProp methods
  • IAttach has no additional methods of its own.

Properties

Required properties Access
PR_ATTACH_METHOD Read/write

Remarks

Called by client applications, IAttach is exposed by attachment objects and is implemented by message store providers. The interface identifier for IAttach is IID_IAttachment, and the pointer type is LPATTACH.

Requirements

Pocket PC: Pocket PC 2002 and later
Smartphone: Smartphone 2002 and later
OS Versions: Windows CE 3.0 and later
Header: mapidefs.h
Library: cemapi.lib
