Best Practices for Developing a Device Driver

Best practices for developing Microsoft Windows CE drivers are recommendations for enhancing your device drivers. Whether a particular best practice applies to your device driver depends on the device driver, the functionality of the device, and how you intend to expose that functionality. Many Windows CE device drivers adhere to these best practices.
