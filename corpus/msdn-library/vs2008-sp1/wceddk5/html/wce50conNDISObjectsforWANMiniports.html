NDIS Objects for WAN Miniports

The following chart summarizes the object identifiers used to get or set operational characteristics for standard WAN miniport drivers.

Length Q S Name
6 M   OID_WAN_PERMANENT_ADDRESS
Permanent station address
6 M   OID_WAN_CURRENT_ADDRESS
Current station
1 or 6   M OID_WAN_PROTOCOL_TYPE
Protocol type
4 M   OID_WAN_MEDIUM_SUBTYPE
Medium subtype
4   M OID_WAN_HEADER_FORMAT
Header format
4 M   OID_WAN_GET_INFO
Get information
4 M   OID_WAN_GET_LINK_INFO
Get link information
4   M OID_WAN_SET_LINK_INFO
Set link information
4 M   OID_WAN_LINE_COUNT
Line count
50 O   OID_WAN_GET_COMP_INFO
Get compression information
50   O OID_WAN_SET_COMP_INFO
Set compression information
60 O   OID_WAN_GET_STATS_INFO
Get statistics information

The OID_WAN_GET_BRIDGE_INFO and OID_WAN_SET_BRIDGE_INFO codes are reserved.
