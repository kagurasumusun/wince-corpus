DVD-Video Renderer

The DVD-Video renderer provides the driver interface required to support DVD-Video and audio playback on Microsoft Windows CE-based devices. DVD-Video is a standard that provides high-quality digital video and audio on the digital video disc (DVD) format. A worldwide group of consumer electronics companies, motion picture studios, and video content makers support the standard.
