Getting Started with XSLT

This guide provides information required for creating XSL Transformations (XSLT) solutions. In addition, it gives you background information to help you make strategic decisions about how to use XSLT for your XML applications. The tutorial will help you get started quickly by guiding you through the process of creating an XSLT solution.
