Line and Closed Figure Drawing

To use the graphics editor tools for drawing lines and closed figures, you place the insertion point at one point and drag to another.

For lines, these points are the endpoints.

For closed figures, these points are the opposite corners of a rectangle bounding the figure.

Lines are drawn in a width determined by the current brush selection, and framed figures are drawn in a width determined by the current width selection.

Lines and all figures, both framed and filled, are drawn in the current foreground color, if you click the left mouse button, or in the current background color, if you click the right mouse button.
