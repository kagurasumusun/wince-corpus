Positioning a Window in Floating Mode Over a Docking Area Without Docking It

You can position a window in floating mode over a docking area without docking it.

To position a window in floating mode over a docking area without docking it

  1. Point to the title bar for the window.
  2. Press CTRL and then drag the window over a docking area of the application window.
