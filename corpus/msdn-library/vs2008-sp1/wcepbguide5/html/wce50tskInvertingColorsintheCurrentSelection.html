Inverting Colors in the Current Selection

You can invert colors in the current selection.

To add multiple controls

  • Choose Invert Colors from the Image menu.

    Colors will be swapped for their opposites, black for white and so on. The graphics editor Colors palette initially displays 16 standard colors. In addition to the displayed colors, you can create custom colors.
