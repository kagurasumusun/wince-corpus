Showing or Hiding a Dockable Window

You can show or hide a dockable window.

To show or hide a dockable window

  • To show a dockable window, from the View menu, choose the window name.

    – or –

    To hide a dockable window, choose the Hide button in the window.

    You can show or hide the Output and Workspace windows at any time; however, you can show or hide debugging windows only during the debugging process.
