Grouping Related Buttons and Menus on a Toolbar

To group related buttons and menus, add a command separator before the first button and after the last button or menu in the group to distinguish the group from other buttons and menus on the toolbar.

To group related buttons and menus on a toolbar

  1. Display the toolbar that you want to group related buttons and menus on.
  2. From the Tools menu, choose Customize and then choose the Toolbars tab.

    The Customize dialog box must remain open; however, you can move it.

  3. Right-click the button you want as the leftmost or top button for the group and then choose Begin a Group from the shortcut menu.
  4. Repeat the preceding step for the first button of the next group to place a command separator at the end of the first group.
  5. To remove the command separator, right-click the first button after the command separator and then clear the Begin a Group check box.
