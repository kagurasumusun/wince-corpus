IntelliSense Option Modification

By default, most IntelliSense options appear automatically. You can turn off any of the automatic options so that they occur on demand. In this case, you invoke them only when you want to, by means of a menu command or key combination.

Even when the IntelliSense options are on by default, you can explicitly invoke them. You can also change the default key mappings for each option.
