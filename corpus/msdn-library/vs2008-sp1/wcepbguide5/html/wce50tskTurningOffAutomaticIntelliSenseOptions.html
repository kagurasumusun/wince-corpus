Turning Off Automatic IntelliSense Options

You can turn off automatic IntelliSense options.

To turn off automatic IntelliSense options

  1. Choose Options from the Tools menu.
  2. Clear the check boxes you do not want on the Editor tab in the Statement Completion Options area.
