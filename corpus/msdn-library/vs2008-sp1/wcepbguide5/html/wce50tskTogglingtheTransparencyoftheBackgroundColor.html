Toggling the Transparency of the Background Color

When you move or copy a selection from a cursor or icon, by default, any pixels in the selection that match the current background color are transparent. That is, they do not obscure pixels in the target location. A custom brush behaves similarly.

If the background is opaque, the existing graphic is obscured by all parts of the selection. If the background is transparent, the existing graphic shows through parts of the selection that match the current background color.

To change the transparency of the background color

  1. Select the appropriate button in the Graphics toolbar option selector.
  2. Choose Draw Opaque from the Image menu.

    - or -

    Clear the Draw Opaque check box on the Image menu.
