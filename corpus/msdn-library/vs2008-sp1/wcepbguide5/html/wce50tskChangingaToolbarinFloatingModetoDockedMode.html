Changing a Toolbar in Floating Mode to Docked Mode

You can change a toolbar in floating mode to docked mode.

To change a toolbar in docked mode to floating mode

  1. Point to the title bar or to a blank area on the toolbar.
  2. Choose from the following:
    • Drag the toolbar to any of the four borders of the application window.

      When you reach the boundary of a docking area with the pointer, the toolbar assumes a shape that is appropriate for the docking location.

      Along the top and bottom borders, it becomes a single horizontal row of buttons; along the right or left borders, it becomes a single vertical row.

    • Double-click the title bar to move the toolbar in floating mode to its last position in docked mode.

      If the toolbar has never been docked, it moves to a new row in the toolbar dock below the menu bar.
