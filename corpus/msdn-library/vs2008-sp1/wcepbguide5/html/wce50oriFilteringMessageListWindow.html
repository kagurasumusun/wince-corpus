Filtering the Message List Window

You can control the results you want to view by filtering the Message List window.

Filters are groups of rules that determine which messages are displayed; presets are groups of filters.

Note   All filters in PREfast are exclusionary. You use filters to tell PREfast what you do not want to see. Any warnings matching the criteria in a particular filter are hidden when the filter is applied.
