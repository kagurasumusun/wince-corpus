Displaying the WizardBar Toolbar Menu

If the WizardBar toolbar menu is hidden, you can display it.

To display the WizardBar toolbar menu

  1. From a WizardBar toolbar combo box, press SHIFT+F10.

    – or –

    From the Action menu, press the DOWN ARROW.

  2. Press ESC if you do not want to choose a command.
