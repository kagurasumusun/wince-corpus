Changing a Toolbar in Docked Mode to Floating Mode

You can change a toolbar in docked mode to floating mode.

To change a toolbar in docked mode to floating mode

  1. Point to a blank area on the toolbar.
  2. Drag the toolbar to the new location.

    – or –

    Double-click the title bar to move the toolbar in docked mode to its previous position in floating mode.
