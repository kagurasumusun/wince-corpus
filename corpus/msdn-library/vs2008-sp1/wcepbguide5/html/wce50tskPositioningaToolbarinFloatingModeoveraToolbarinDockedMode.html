Positioning a Toolbar in Floating Mode over a Toolbar in Docked Mode

You can position a toolbar in floating mode over a toolbar in docked mode.

To position a toolbar in floating mode over a toolbar in docked mode

  1. Point to the title bar or to a blank area on the toolbar.
  2. Press CTRL and then drag the toolbar over a docking area of the application window.

    The orientation of a toolbar in docked mode generally corresponds to the orientation of the docking area. Toolbars dock vertically on vertical docking areas and horizontally on horizontal docking areas.

  3. To switch a toolbar's docked orientation between horizontal and vertical, press or release the SHIFT key as you drag the toolbar.
