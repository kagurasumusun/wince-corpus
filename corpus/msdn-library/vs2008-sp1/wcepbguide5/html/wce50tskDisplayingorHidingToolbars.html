Displaying or Hiding Toolbars

You can display or hide toolbars in the IDE.

To display or hide toolbars

  1. From the Tools menu, choose Customize and then choose the Toolbars tab.
  2. Select any toolbars you want to display.

    - or -

    Clear any toolbars you want to hide.

Platform Builder displays each toolbar in its default location or in its last location, if you moved it.
