Moving to the Next or Previous Bookmark

After you set bookmarks in a file, you can move to the next or previous bookmark.

To move to the next or previous bookmark

  • Use the key combination you have chosen to invoke the BookmarkNext command to move to the next bookmark.

    – or –

    Use the key combination you have chosen to invoke the BookmarkPrev command to move to the previous bookmark.
