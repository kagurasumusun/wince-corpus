Platform Builder for Microsoft Windows CE 5.0  

ARM Technology Guide

ARM Ltd licenses its reduced instruction-set computing (RISC) microprocessor, peripheral, and system-chip designs to leading international electronics companies.

Platform Builder for Microsoft® Windows® CE provides several sample board support packages that are based on ARM microprocessors. In addition, intrinsic functions and compiler options are available to take advantage of the special features of ARM microprocessors.

Over 70 semiconductor companies license ARM CPU technology.
