Hiding or Displaying ClassView

You can hide or display the ClassView tab by performing one of the following actions.

To hide or display the ClassView tab

  • Right-click in an unused area of the Workspace window and then switch the display by selecting or clearing ClassView from the list of views.
  • From the View menu, choose Class View to switch the display of the ClassView tab.
