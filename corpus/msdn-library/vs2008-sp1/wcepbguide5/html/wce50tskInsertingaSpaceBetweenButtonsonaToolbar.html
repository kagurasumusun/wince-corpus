Inserting a Space Between Buttons on a Toolbar

You can insert a space between buttons on a toolbar.

To insert a space between toolbar buttons

  1. To insert a space before a button that is not followed by a space, drag the button to the right or down until it overlaps the next button halfway.
  2. To insert a space before a button that is followed by a space and retain the space following the button, drag the button until the right or bottom edge touches the next button or slightly overlaps it.
  3. To insert a space before a button that is followed by a space and close up the following space, drag the button to the right or down until it overlaps the next button halfway.
