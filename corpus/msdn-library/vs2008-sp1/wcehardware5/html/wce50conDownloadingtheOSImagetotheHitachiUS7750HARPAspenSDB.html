Downloading the Run-Time Image to the Renesas US7750R HARP (Aspen) SDB

If the Window CE boot loader is downloaded to the Renesas US7750R HARP (Aspen) SDB, you can download the run-time image.

To download the run-time image to the Renesas US7750R HARP (Aspen) SDB

  1. Turn off the Aspen SDB and verify that the DIP switch S1-1 is in the Off position.
  2. Turn on the Aspen SDB and download the run-time image.

    For more information, see Downloading a Run-Time Image.
