Modifying the SMDK2410 Samsung MCU Development Kit Switches and Jumpers

To execute the boot loader code, modify the switches and jumpers on the SMDK2410 Samsung MCU Development Kit as indicated in the Development Kit documentation.

To modify the SMDK2410 Samsung MCU Development Kit switches and jumpers

  • Verify that each switch is in the correct On or Off position.

    The following table shows the correct switch and jumper settings.

    J16 J17 J33 J34
    1:2 2:3 Off On
