Setting up the Broadcom VoIP Screen Phone Hardware

Before you can download the Windows CE boot loader to your Broadcom VoIP Screen Phone, you must set up and connect the phone to a development workstation.

To set up the Broadcom VoIP Screen Phone

  1. Connect the handset to the phone base.
  2. Plug one end of the Ethernet cable into an open network port, and plug the other end into the phone base.
  3. Connect the power adapter to the phone, and then plug the adapter into an electrical outlet.

    Make sure the power indicator LED is lit.

  4. Connect one end of the serial cable to your development workstation and connect the other end to the phone.
