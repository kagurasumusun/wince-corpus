Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC

The Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 are similar Geode-based single board computers. The PCM-5822 has one Ethernet connection and the PCM-5823 has two Ethernet connections. Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC Hardware Limitations

For non-PCI or built-in devices, some Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC components may need to be disabled in order to allow for IRQ support on some devices. For more information, see the Advantech PCM-5822 and PCM-5823 Biscuit PC documentation.
