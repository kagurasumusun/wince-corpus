Choosing an Internet Browser

Microsoft® Windows® CE offers a choice of two browsers, Internet Explorer 6 for Windows CE and the mobile Internet browser, Microsoft Pocket Internet Explorer. Each of these browsers is optimized for different device requirements. To help you make the best choice for your particular OS design, the topics in this section discuss the advantages and tradeoffs inherent in each browser.
