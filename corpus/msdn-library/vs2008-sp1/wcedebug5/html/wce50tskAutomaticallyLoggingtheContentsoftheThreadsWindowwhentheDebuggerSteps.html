Automatically Logging the Contents of the Threads Window when the Debugger Steps

You can have the contents of the Threads window automatically saved in a log file as the debugger steps through a function.

To automatically log the contents of the Threads window when the debugger steps

  • On the Threads window toolbar, choose the Auto Log on Step button.

    - or -

    Right-click the Threads window and then choose Auto Log on Step from the shortcut menu.

    Note   Auto Refresh on Step is automatically enabled when you choose Auto Log on Step.
