Logging the Contents of the Processes Window

You can log the contents of a processes window.

To log the contents of the Processes window

  • On the Processes window toolbar, choose the Log button.

    - or -

    Right-click the Processes window and then choose Log from the shortcut menu.

    The data in the window is formatted into rows and columns and written to the Log tab of the Output window.
