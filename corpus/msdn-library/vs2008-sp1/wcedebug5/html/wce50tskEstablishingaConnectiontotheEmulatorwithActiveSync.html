Establishing a Connection to the Emulator with ActiveSync

You can establish a connection to the Emulator with Microsoft® ActiveSync® 3.5 or later. You cannot establish a connection with earlier versions of ActiveSync.

To configure the Emulator to establish a connection with ActiveSync

  1. Connect two serial ports with a null modem cable.
  2. On your development workstation, install ActiveSync.
  3. From the Target menu, choose Connectivity Options.
  4. Choose Kernel Service Map.
  5. From the Download box, choose Emulator, and then choose Settings.
  6. If your run-time image supports KITL, from the Serial Port 2 box choose COM1 or COM2, and then from the Serial Port 1 box choose None.

    - or -

    If your run-time image does not support KITL, from the Serial Port 1 box choose COM1 or COM2, and then from the Serial Port 2 box choose None.

  7. Choose OK, and then choose Apply.
  8. Configure ActiveSync to connect to the serial port — either COM1 or COM2 — that you did not assign to the Emulator.
