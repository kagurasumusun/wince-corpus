Automatically Refresh the Contents of the Modules and Symbols Window on Step

You can have the contents of the Modules and Symbols window automatically refreshed as the debugger steps through a function.

To have the contents of the Modules and Symbols windows automatically refresh on step

  • In the Modules and Symbols window, right-click the list of modules and then choose Auto Refresh on Step from the shortcut menu.

    – or –

    On the window toolbar, choose the Auto Refresh on Step button.

    The Modules and Symbols window refreshes when the debugger takes a step.
