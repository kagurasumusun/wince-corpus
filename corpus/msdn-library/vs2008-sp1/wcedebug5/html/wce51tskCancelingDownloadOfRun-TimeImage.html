Canceling the Download of a Run-Time Image

You can cancel the download of a run-time image to a target device by performing the following command in the integrated development environment (IDE).

To cancel the download of a run-time image

  • Choose from the following:
    • From the Target menu, choose Detach Device.

      This detaches the target device from your development workstation. You must reattach the device to establish a connection for running connectivity services.

    • In the dialog box that displays a progress indicator for the download, choose Cancel.
