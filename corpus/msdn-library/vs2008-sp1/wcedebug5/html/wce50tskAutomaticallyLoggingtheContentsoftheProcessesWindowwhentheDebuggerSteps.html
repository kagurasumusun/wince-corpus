Automatically Logging the Contents of the Processes Window when the Debugger Steps

You can automatically log the Processes window after the debugger stops.

To automatically log the contents of the Processes window when the debugger steps

  • On the Processes window toolbar, choose the Auto Log on Step button.

    - or -

    Right-click the Processes window and then choose Auto Log on Step from the shortcut menu.

    Note   Auto Refresh on Step is enabled when you choose Auto Log on Step.
