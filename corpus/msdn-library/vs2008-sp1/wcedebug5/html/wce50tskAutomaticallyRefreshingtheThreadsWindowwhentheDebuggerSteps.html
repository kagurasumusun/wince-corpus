Automatically Refreshing the Threads Window when the Debugger Steps

You can have the threads window automatically refreshed as the debugger steps through a function.

To automatically refresh the Threads window when the debugger steps

  • On the Threads window toolbar, choose the Auto Refresh on Step button.

    - or -

    Right-click the Threads window and then choose Auto Refresh on Step from the shortcut menu.

    The Threads window refreshes when the debugger steps.
