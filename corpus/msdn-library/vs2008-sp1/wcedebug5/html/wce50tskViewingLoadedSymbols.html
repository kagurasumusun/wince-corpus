Viewing Loaded Symbols

You can view the symbols that have been loaded.

To view loaded symbols

  1. Start a debugging session.
  2. From the Target menu, choose CE Modules and Symbols.

    – or –

    From the View menu, choose Debug Windows and then choose Modules and Symbols.
