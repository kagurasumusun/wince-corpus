Refreshing the Contents of the Call Stack Window

You can refresh the contents of the Call Stack window with the most recent values.

To refresh the contents of the Call Stack window

  • Right-click the Call Stack window and then choose Refresh from the shortcut menu.

    – or –

    On the Call Stack window toolbar, choose the Refresh button.
