Battery Life Debugging Techniques

Debugging problems related to battery life typically requires that you use a combination of hardware and software to isolate the cause of the problems. In many cases, the most valuable hardware tool is a current meter, which enables you to monitor power consumption directly on the device in real time. The most useful software tools are often the debugger and the call profiler.
