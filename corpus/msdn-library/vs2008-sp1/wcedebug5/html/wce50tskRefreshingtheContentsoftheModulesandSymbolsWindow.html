Refreshing the Contents of the Modules and Symbols Window

You can refresh the contents of the Modules and Symbols window with the most recent information.

To refresh the contents of the Modules and Symbols window

  • In the Modules and Symbols window, right-click the list of modules and then choose Refresh on the shortcut menu.

    – or –

    On the window toolbar, choose the Refresh button.

    If the data in the Modules and Symbols window is fresh, the Refresh button is disabled.

    If the data is outdated, the Refresh button is enabled.

    Note   Refresh does not load or unload symbols.
