System Troubleshooting with Remote Kernel Tracker

The Remote Kernel Tracker tool provides you with functionality that can assist you in troubleshooting a number of conditions in your system.

The tool displays a detailed picture of the interactions between threads on your target device. As a result, the tool can assist you in dealing with problems related to thread interactions.

Some conditions in your system, such as a memory leak or a missed real-time deadline, can be difficult to pinpoint and analyze with a traditional development tool or kernel level debugger. With Remote Kernel Tracker, you can diagnose and analyze conditions such as these in a straightforward manner.
