Unloading All Symbols in the Modules and Symbols Window

You can unload all the symbols in the Modules and Symbols window.

To unload all symbols in the Modules and Symbols window

  • In the Modules and Symbols window, right-click a module and then choose Unload All Symbols from the shortcut menu.

    - or -

    Choose the Unload All Symbols button on the window toolbar.
