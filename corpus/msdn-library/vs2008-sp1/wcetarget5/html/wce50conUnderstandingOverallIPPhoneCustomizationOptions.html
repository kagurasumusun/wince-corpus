Understanding Overall IP Phone Customization Options

The VoIP functionality in Windows CE is designed to be customizable in many ways. You can often customize the operation of a particular piece of functionality and if the customization does not meet your needs, you can usually replace the functionality with your own code or with functionality you obtain elsewhere.

For detailed information about customizing the major pieces of functionality, see Customizing IP Phone Functionality.
