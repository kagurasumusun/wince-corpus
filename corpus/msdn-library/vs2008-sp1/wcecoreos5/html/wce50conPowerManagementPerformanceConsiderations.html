Power Management Performance Considerations

When you create an OS design that includes power management, you should pay attention to areas of power management that may affect the power management performance of your OS design. The following table shows some considerations to keep in mind as you create an OS design that includes power management.

Considerations Topic
Power management framework. With the framework, a device driver can regulate device power. Power Management Architecture
Power management restrictions on device power consumption. Device Power Management Guidelines
Power consumption in periods of inactivity. Device Power Self-Management
Power management clients. Power Manager Interfaces
Power management communication with device drivers. Device Driver Interface
Power management interaction with applications. Application Interface
