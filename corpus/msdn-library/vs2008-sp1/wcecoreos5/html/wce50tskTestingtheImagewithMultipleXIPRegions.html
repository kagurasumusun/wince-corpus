Testing the Run-Time Image with Multiple XIP Regions

After you have successfully downloaded and booted your run-time image with multiple XIP regions, you can test your run-time image to verify that the modules you have placed in SHELL loaded correctly.

To test your run-time image with multiple XIP Regions

  1. On your CEPC, double-click on My Device.
  2. Verify that the file system has loaded correctly by making sure that the following folders are present:
    • Application Data
    • My Documents
    • Profiles
    • Release
    • Temp
    • Windows
