COleControl::GetClientOffset

This method retrieves he difference between the upper-left corner of the control rectangular area of the control and the upper-left corner of its client area.

virtual void GetClientOffset(
long* pdxOffset,
long* pdyOffset )
const;

Parameters

pdxOffset
Specifies a pointer to the horizontal offset of the OLE control client area.
pdyOffset
Specifies a pointer to the vertical offset of the OLE control client area.

Requirements

  Windows CE versions: 2.0 and later  
  Header file: Declared in Afxctl.h
  Platform: H/PC Pro, Palm-size PC, Pocket PC
