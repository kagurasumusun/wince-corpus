CNotSupportedException

The CNotSupportedException class encapsulates an exception that is the result of a request for an unsupported feature. No further qualification is necessary or possible.

Requirements

  Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later  
  Header file: Declared in Afx.h
  Platform: H/PC Pro, Palm-size PC, Pocket PC
