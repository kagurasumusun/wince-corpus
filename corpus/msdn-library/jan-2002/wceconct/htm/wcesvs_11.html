Preparing for Remote Connection

If users want to establish a remote connection between their desktop computer and their device, ActiveSync helps them prepare their device for the remote connection. They follow a series of steps to set up a dial-up connection to a modem, a dial-up connection to a network, or a network (Ethernet) connection.
