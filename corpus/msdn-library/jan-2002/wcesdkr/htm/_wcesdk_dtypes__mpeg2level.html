MPEG2Level

Indicates the MPEG-2 video level as specified in the MPEG-2 video standard. (ISO13818-2) 

enum MPEG2Level {
MPEG2Level_Low = 1,
MPEG2Level_Main,
MPEG2Level_High1440,
MPEG2Level_High
};

Elements

MPEG2Level_Low
Low level.
MPEG2Level_Main
Main level.
MPEG2Level_High1440
High level.
MPEG2Level_High
Highest level.
