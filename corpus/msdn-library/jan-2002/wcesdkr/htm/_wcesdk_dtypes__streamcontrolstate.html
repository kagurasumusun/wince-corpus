StreamControlState

Specifies the state of the stream. 

enum StreamControlState {
STREAM_FLOWING,
STREAM_DISCARDING
};

Elements

STREAM_FLOWING
Indicates the stream is currently transmitting data.
STREAM_DISCARDING
Discards the samples as they are received.
