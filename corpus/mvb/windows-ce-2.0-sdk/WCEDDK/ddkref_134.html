NdisEqualString

NdisEqualString compares two strings, in the OS-default character set, to determine whether they are equal.

Syntax

BOOLEAN NdisEqualString(IN PNDIS_STRING String1, IN PNDIS_STRING String2, IN BOOLEAN CaseInsensitive);
