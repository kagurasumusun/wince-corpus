2.4 Reserved Words

Reserved words are names that the assembler reserves as symbols with special meanings. The assembler uses the following reserved words, without regard to upper or lower case:

Table 2.1 Reserved Words

Type
Reserved Words
Register names
R0, R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R8, R9, R10, R11, R12, R13, R14, R15, FP*(4), SP*(5), SR, SP, GBR, VBR, MACH, MACL, PR, PC, GBR, VBR, SSR, SPC, R0_BANK, R1_BANK, R2_BANK, R3_BANK, R4_BANK, R5_BANK, R6_BANK, R7_BANK, R0_USER*(6), R1_USER*(6), R2_USER*(6), R3_USER*(6), R4_USER*(6), R5_USER*(6), R6_USER*(6), R7_USER*(6)

Operators(1)
STARTOF, SIZEOF
Symbols(3)


The location counter symbol*(2), and the dollar sign ($: a single character symbol)
