Count Register (9)

The read/write Count register acts as a timer, incrementing at a constant rate— same the MasterOut clock speed—whether or not instructions are being executed, retired, or any forward progress is actually made through the pipeline. When the register reaches all ones, it rolls over to zero and continues counting. This register can be written for diagnostic purposes or system initialization.
