3.2.2 Divide Instruction (DIV, DIVU)

The PR3910 processor core contains a division module independent of the pipeline execution. Division starts from the pipeline alu stage and takes 35 cycles.

Note: When an MTHI, MTLO, DIV or DIVU instruction comes up for execution when a DIV or DIV or DIVU instruction is already being executed in progress, the PR3910 will stop the DIV or DIVU in progress and will begin executing the MTHI, MTLO or new DIV or DIVU instruction The PR3910 processor core will not halt execution of a DIV or DIVU instruction when an exception occurs during its execution.

Division stops in Halt and Doze mode. It restarts when the PR3910 returns from Halt or Doze mode.
