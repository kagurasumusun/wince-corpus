BGTZ Branch On Greater Than Zero

Format : BGTZ rs,offset

Description : A branch target address is computed from the sum of the address of the instruction in the delay slot and the 16-bit offset, shifted left two bits and sign-extended to 32 bits. The contents of general register rs are compared to zero. If the contents of general register rs have the sign bit cleared and are not equal to zero, then the program branches to the target address, with a delay of one instruction.
