SLLV Shift Left Logical Variable

Format : SLLV rd,rt,rs

Description : The contents of general register rt are shifted left by the number of bits specified by the low-order 5 bits of the contents of general register rs, inserting zeroes into the low order bits. The 32-bit result is placed in register rd.

Operation :

T: GPR[rd] GPR[rt](31-GPR[rs]4..0)..0 || 0GPR [rs]4..0

Exceptions : None.
