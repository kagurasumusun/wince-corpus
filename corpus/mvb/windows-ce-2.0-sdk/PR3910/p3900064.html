7.1 PR3910 Instruction Set

The PR3910 processor family instruction set has five opcode groups. These groups use fields in the instruction which may be separate or overlapping. Four of opcode groups are extensions of the major opcode field.

The MAJOR opcodes use bits 31 to 26 of the instruction word as shown in 1. The encoding of the instructions is broken down into two sub-fields. These are the row and column axes of the opcode table. The column heading of the MAJOR opcode table use hex format for the remaining two bits on the opcode field. This code would be different is a disassembled instruction were being examined.

The SPECIAL opcode group is used when the MAJOR opcode is 0x00 hex. This opcode group uses the lower six bits of the instruction word for addition encoding. The SPECIAL opcode group is shown in 1.

The BCOND opcode group is for additional branch conditions. The group is used when the MAJOR opcode is 0x01 hex. The BCOND group uses bits 20 to 16 in the instruction word and is shown in 1.

The last two groups are for coprocessor instructions. These are the COPz group and the COP0 group. The COP0 group is a subset of the COPz group but uses a separate field in the instruction word. The COPz group uses bits 25 to 21 and bit 16 as an additional field to encode the instructions. The COP0 group also uses bits 4 to 0. The COPz group is shown in 1 and the COP0 group is shown in 1.
