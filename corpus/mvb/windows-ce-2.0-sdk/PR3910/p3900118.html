MFHI Move From HI

Format : MFHI rd

Description : The contents of special register HI are loaded into general register rd. To ensure proper operation in the event of interruptions, the two instructions which follow a MFHI instruction may not be any of the instructions which modify the HI register: MULT, MULTU, DIV, DIVU, MADD, MADDU, MTHI.

Operation :

T: GPR[rd] HI

Exceptions : None.
