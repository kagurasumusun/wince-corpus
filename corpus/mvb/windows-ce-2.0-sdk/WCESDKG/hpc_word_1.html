Recognized Tokens

The RTF tokens recognized by Microsoft Pocket Word 2.0 form a subset of the full set of RTF tokens.

The tokens recognized by Microsoft Pocket Word 2.0 fall into the following three categories:

  • Tokens that produce specified RTF behavior

  • Tokens that are treated as other recognized tokens

  • Tokens that are preserved but have little or no effect on the display

For a list of the RTF tokens supported by Pocket Word 2.0, see Fully Recognized Tokens. Refer to RTF specification documentation for information on their usage.
