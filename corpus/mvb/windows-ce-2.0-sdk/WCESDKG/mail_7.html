Working with Messages

You can follow the general procedure for working with messages.

  • Open the message store with MailOpen.

  • Perform tasks:

    Retrieving messages

    Inserting messages

    Sending messages

    Copying and moving messages

    Updating Message Store

  • If MailFirst, MailGet, or MailNext was used in the step above, free the heap with MailFree.

  • Close the message store with MailClose.
