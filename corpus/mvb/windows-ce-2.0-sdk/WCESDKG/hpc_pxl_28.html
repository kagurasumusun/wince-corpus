Microsoft Pocket Excel Formulas

Formulas in Pocket Excel are a perfect subset of the formulas in Desktop Excel 95 files. Refer to the "Microsoft Excel Formulas" section in Chapter 3 of the Excel 95 Developer's Kit for detailed information on Excel formulas. The following is a list of parse things that are supported by Pocket Excel:

ptgAdd ptgSub ptgMul ptgDiv ptgPower ptgConcat ptgInt ptgNum ptgStr ptgErr ptgBool ptgRefErr ptgRefErrV ptgRef ptgRefV ptgRefN ptgRefNV ptgLT ptgLE ptgEQ ptgGE ptgGT ptgNE ptgUminus ptgPercent ptgUplus ptgParen ptgMissArg ptgFunc ptgFuncV ptgFuncVar ptgFuncVarV ptgAreaErr ptgAreaErrV ptgArea ptgAreaV ptgAreaN ptgNameX ptgNameXV ptgName ptgNameV ptgNameA ptgRef3d ptgRef3dV ptgRefErr3d ptgArea3d ptgArea3dV ptgAreaErr3d

ptgAttr (only bitFAttrIf, bitFAttrGoto, bitFAttrSum and bitFAttrChoose)
