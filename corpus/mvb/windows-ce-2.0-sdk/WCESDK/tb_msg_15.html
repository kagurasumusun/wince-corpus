TB_GETITEMRECT

The TB_GETITEMRECT message retrieves the bounding rectangle of a button in a toolbar.

Syntax

TB_GETITEMRECT
wParam = (WPARAM) iButton;
lParam = (LPARAM)(LPRECT) lprc;
