COMMANDBANDSRESTOREINFO

The COMMANDBANDSRESTOREINFO structure contains information that is necessary to restore a band to its current state.

Syntax

typedef struct tagCOMMANDBANDSRESTOREINFO {
UINT cbSize;
UINT wID;
UINT fStyle;
UINT cxRestored;
BOOL fMaximized;
} COMMANDBANDSRESTOREINFO, *LPCOMMANDBANDSRESTOREINFO;

At a Glance

Header file:
Component:
Platforms: H/PC
Versions: 2.0 and later

Members

dwSize
Size of the structure, in bytes. You must supply the value for this field.
wID
Identifier of the band. This field corresponds to the wID field of the REBARBANDINFO structure. This field is filled in by the CommandBands_GetRestoreInformation function.
fStyle
Flags that specify the band style. This field corresponds to the fStyle field of the REBARBANDINFO structure. This field is filled in by the CommandBands_GetRestoreInformation function. The value can be a combination of the following.
RBBS_BREAK
The band is on a new line.
RBBS_CHILDEDGE
The band has an edge at the top and bottom of the child window.
RBBS_FIXEDBMP
The background bitmap does not move when the band is resized.
RBBS_FIXEDSIZE
The band can't be sized. With this style, the sizing grip is not displayed on the band.
RBBS_GRIPPERALWAYS
The band will always have sizing grip, even if it is the only band in the rebar.
RBBS_HIDDEN
The band will not be visible.
RBBS_NOVERT
The band won't be displayed when the rebar control uses the CCS_VERT style.
RBBS_VARIABLEHEIGHT
The band can be resized by the rebar control. cyIntegral and cyMaxChild affect how the rebar will resize the band.

cxRestored
Restored width of the band, in pixels. The restored width is the width of the band after the user has dragged it. If the user has never dragged it, it is the initial size of the band. This field corresponds to the cx field of the REBARBANDINFO structure. This field is filled in by the CommandBands_GetRestoreInformation function.
fMaximized
Indicates whether the band is maximized. Only one band in a row can be maximized at a time. This field corresponds to the fStyle field of the REBARBANDINFO structure. This field is filled in by the CommandBands_GetRestoreInformation function.
