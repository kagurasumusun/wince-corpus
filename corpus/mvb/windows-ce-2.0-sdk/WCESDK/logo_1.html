What Does the Designed for Microsoft Windows CE Logo Mean?

The Designed for Microsoft Windows CE Logo Program was developed by Microsoft to help end users easily identify desktop hardware and software products that were designed specifically for the Microsoft Windows CE operating system. Users can mix and match hardware and software products with the Windows CE Logo and be assured that the products will take advantage of the new technologies integrated into this innovative operating system. As a software vendor, licensing the Logo enables you to use the Logo on your product packaging, advertising, collateral, and other marketing materials. This signals to customers that:

  • Your product is fully functional on Microsoft Windows CE-compatible devices; and

  • You designed your product to take advantage of the unique benefits of running on Microsoft Windows CE-based devices.

In addition, the Logo will be a requirement for participating in many joint marketing opportunities with Microsoft Corporation.
