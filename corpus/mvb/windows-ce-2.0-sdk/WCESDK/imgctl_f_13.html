ImageList_GetDragImage

The ImageList_GetDragImage function retrieves the temporary image list that is used for the drag image. ImageList_GetDragImage also retrieves the current drag position, and the offset of the drag image relative to the drag position.

Syntax

HIMAGELIST WINAPI ImageList_GetDragImage(POINT FAR* ppt, POINT FAR* pptHotspot);

At a Glance

Component: Imgctl
Platforms: H/PC
