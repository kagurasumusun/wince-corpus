HDN_GETDISPINFO

The HDN_GETDISPINFO notification message is sent to the owner of a header control when the control needs information about a callback header item. This notification is sent as a WM_NOTIFY message.

Syntax

HDN_GETDISPINFO
pDispInfo = (LPNMHDDISPINFO) lParam;

At a Glance

Header file:
Component/Module:
Platforms:
Versions: 2.0 and later

Parameters

pDispInfo
Address of an NMHDDISPINFO structure. On input, the fields of the structure specify what information is required and the item of interest.

Remarks

Fill the appropriate fields of the structure to return the requested information to the header control. If your message handler sets the mask field of the NMHDDISPINFO structure to HDI_DI_SETITEM, the header control stores the information and will not request it again.
